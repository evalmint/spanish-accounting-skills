## How the size test works

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Two of the three criteria must be met at the closing date | LSC arts. 257.1, 258.1, 263.2 |
| The right is gained when the criteria are met at two consecutive closing dates, and lost when they are failed at two consecutive closing dates | Same |
| First year after incorporation, transformation or merger: test only at that first closing date | LSC arts. 257.2, 258.2, 263.3; RD 1515/2007 arts. 2.1, 4.1 |
| Activo = total of the balance-sheet model | PGC 3rd part, norma 4.ª.1 |
| Cifra de negocios = importe neto (net of discounts and IVA) | Cco art. 35.2 |
| Employees = average headcount during the year | LSC art. 257.1.c |
| Groups (Cco art. 42): aggregate activo, cifra and headcount of the group, with consolidation eliminations, for PGC abbreviated models and PGC-PYMES; not applied when the company's figures are included in the parent's consolidated accounts (PGC abbreviated rule) | PGC norma 4.ª.1; RD 1515/2007 art. 2.1 |
| PGC-PYMES: figures measured under the framework applied in the last year | RD 1515/2007 art. 2.1 |
| EIPs cannot use abbreviated models or PGC-PYMES | PGC norma 4.ª.3; RD 1515/2007 art. 2.2.a |

The audit test (LSC art. 263) has no group-aggregation rule in its text; it is applied to the company's own figures.

## All thresholds in one table

Valid on 2026-11-24 (no change since RD 602/2016 and Ley 22/2015). The consolidation exemption in Cco art. 43.1.1.ª is tested on the group's last accounts at the parent's closing date, without the two-year wording.

| Purpose | Activo ≤ | Cifra de negocios ≤ | Avg. employees ≤ | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Microempresa criteria (PGC-PYMES users) | 1,000,000 € | 2,000,000 € | 10 | RD 1515/2007 art. 4.1 |
| No audit | 2,850,000 € | 5,700,000 € | 50 | LSC art. 263.2 |
| Balance, ECPN, memoria abreviados; no EFE; no informe de gestión | 4,000,000 € | 8,000,000 € | 50 | LSC arts. 257, 262.3 |
| PGC-PYMES | 4,000,000 € | 8,000,000 € | 50 | RD 1515/2007 art. 2.1 |
| PyG abreviada; no supplier-payment note in the informe de gestión; group exempt from consolidating | 11,400,000 € | 22,800,000 € | 250 | LSC arts. 258, 262.1; Cco art. 43.1.1.ª |
| EINF required (above): more than 250 employees AND (EIP, or one of activo > 20 M€ / cifra > 40 M€ for two years) | > 20,000,000 € | > 40,000,000 € | > 250 | Ley 11/2018 DT.3 |

## Worked example

An SL with these closing figures (no group):

| Year | Activo | Cifra | Employees | Criteria met for abbreviated balance (4 / 8 / 50) | For no audit (2.85 / 5.7 / 50) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | 3.1 M€ | 6.2 M€ | 38 | 3 of 3 | 1 of 3 (employees) |
| 2025 | 3.6 M€ | 7.4 M€ | 44 | 3 of 3 | 1 of 3 |
| 2026 | 4.3 M€ | 8.6 M€ | 47 | 1 of 3 | 1 of 3 |

- 2026: may still use abbreviated balance and PGC-PYMES, because it fails the test only at one closing date; it loses the right if 2027 also fails.
- Audit: in 2024 and 2025 only one criterion was met, so the company failed the exemption at two consecutive closing dates. Assuming it was exempt before 2024, it must audit 2025 and stays obliged until it meets two of three at two consecutive closing dates again. Check the history before 2024.

## Audit: when it is required

| Trigger | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Size | Exceeds two of 2.85 M€ / 5.7 M€ / 50 at two consecutive closing dates | LSC art. 263 |
| Listed securities, public bond issues, financial intermediaries, insurers, pension funds | Always | Ley 22/2015 DA 1.ª.1.a-d |
| Public subsidies or aid | Over 600,000 € accumulated received in a year (Ley 38/2003 art. 2 definition): audit that year and the years in which the subsidised operations or investments are carried out | Ley 22/2015 DA 1.ª.1.e; RD 2/2021 DA 2.ª |
| Public-sector contracts | Contracts under Ley 9/2017 art. 2 over 600,000 € accumulated in a year and over 50 % of cifra de negocios: audit that year and the next | RD 2/2021 DA 3.ª |
| Minority request | Partners with ≥ 5 % of capital may ask the Registro Mercantil to appoint an auditor at the company's cost, within 3 months of year-end, if the company is not otherwise obliged | LSC art. 265.2 |
| Statutes or voluntary | If the bylaws require it, or the junta decides; a voluntary audit whose auditor is registered at the Registro Mercantil is deposited with the accounts | LSC art. 279.1 |
| Court or registrar order | At the request of a person with a legitimate interest | Cco art. 40 |

The auditor reports on the cuentas anuales and checks that the informe de gestión is consistent with them (LSC art. 263.1; Ley 22/2015 art. 5).

## Appointing the auditor

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| The junta appoints the auditor before the end of the year to be audited, for an initial term of 3-9 years | LSC art. 264.1 |
| Cannot be removed in the initial term without just cause | LSC art. 264.3 |
| If the junta did not appoint in time, administrators or any partner can ask the registrar to appoint one | LSC art. 265.1 |
| Appointments for DA 1.ª-3.ª triggers (subsidies, contracts) also before year-end | RD 2/2021 DA 4.ª |
| The appointment is registered at the Registro Mercantil | RRM (registration of auditors) |
| The auditor has at least one month from receiving the signed accounts | LSC art. 270.1 |

If an audited company files without the audit report, the registrar refuses the deposit (LSC art. 279.1 lists the report among the documents).

## Status of the threshold reform

Delegated Directive (EU) 2023/2775 raised EU size criteria by about 25 % (small: 7.5 M€ balance / 15 M€ turnover; medium: 25 M€ / 50 M€), with a transposition deadline of 24-12-2024. Spain's bill (Proyecto de Ley 121/000075, BOCG 21-11-2025) proposed: abbreviated balance and PGC-PYMES 7.5 M€ / 15 M€ / 50; audit 3.565 M€ / 7.125 M€ / 50. The bill was declared lapsed (*caducado*) on 6-10-2026 (BOCG D-610, 4-11-2026). Until a new law is published in the BOE, use the thresholds above.
