## Approval by the junta

- The junta general approves the accounts (LSC art. 272.1) and resolves on the application of profit according to the approved balance (art. 273.1). In a sociedad unipersonal the sole shareholder decides and the decision is recorded in an acta (LSC art. 15).
- A *junta universal* (all capital present and unanimously accepting) needs no prior call (LSC art. 178).
- From the call, any partner may obtain the accounts, informe de gestión and audit report immediately and free of charge (art. 272.2). In an SL, partners with ≥ 5 % may examine the supporting documents at the registered office with an expert, unless the bylaws say otherwise (art. 272.3).
- The resolution and its date go into the libro de actas (Cco art. 26).

## Registrar review and publicity

- The registrar checks within 15 days that the documents are those required, duly approved and signed (LSC art. 280.1). Defects are notified and can be corrected; the filing entry lasts a limited time (RRM art. 378.2).
- Deposited documents are public: anyone can obtain them (LSC art. 281). The registry keeps them for 6 years (art. 280.2).
- Any voluntary publication says whether it is full or abbreviated (LSC art. 284).

## Not depositing: cierre registral and fines

| Consequence | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Cierre registral | No document about the company is registered while the failure persists | LSC art. 282.1 |
| Exceptions | Removal or resignation of administrators, managers or liquidators; revocation or renunciation of powers; dissolution and appointment of liquidators; court or administrative orders | LSC art. 282.2; RRM art. 378.1 |
| When the registrar applies it | One year after the year-end without a deposit of approved accounts | RRM art. 378.1 |
| Accounts not approved | No closing if, before the year passes, a certificate of the administrative body (legitimised signatures) or a notarial acta shows the junta did not approve them; renew every 6 months | RRM art. 378.5 |
| ICAC fine | 1,200 € to 60,000 €; up to 300,000 € per year of delay if the company or group turnover exceeds 6,000,000 €; graded by assets and sales from the last tax return, or by capital | LSC art. 283.1-2 |
| Reduction | If deposited before the sanction procedure starts: minimum grade, reduced by 50 % | LSC art. 283.3 |
| Prescription | 3 years | LSC art. 283.4 |
| Tax link | BINs older than 10 years can only be proved with accounts whose deposit is shown | LIS art. 26.5 |
| Administrator liability | A long failure to file can feed claims against administrators in insolvency (unverified as a general rule; fact-specific) | |

To lift a cierre registral, deposit the missing approved accounts (or file the non-approval certificate of RRM art. 378.5). How many past years the registrar requires was not verified here; ask the registry.

## Checklist

1. Confirm the model per document (normal, abreviado, PYMES) with two years of figures (`auditoria-y-umbrales.md`).
2. Formulated and signed by every administrator within 3 months, with the date.
3. Audit report attached if required; auditor registered.
4. Junta called with the right notice, or universal; acta in the libro de actas.
5. Certificate of approval and application of profit; signatures legitimised or electronic.
6. Official forms complete: identification, titularidad real, CNAE-2025.
7. Filed within 1 month of approval; keep the filing receipt.
8. The user signs and files; the agent prepares only.
