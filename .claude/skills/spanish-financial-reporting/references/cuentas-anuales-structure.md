## The five documents

Cco art. 34.1, LSC art. 254 and PGC 3rd part, norma 1.ª: balance, cuenta de pérdidas y ganancias (PyG), estado de cambios en el patrimonio neto (ECPN), estado de flujos de efectivo (EFE) and memoria. They form a unit and must give the *imagen fiel* (true and fair view). Structure follows the official models (LSC art. 254.3): items appear separately in the given order (art. 255.1), may be subdivided or added if not covered (255.2), and immaterial items may be grouped if disclosed in the memoria (art. 256). Each figure shows the prior year (Cco art. 35.6). The structure may not change from year to year except in justified exceptional cases (Cco art. 35.8). Amounts in euros, or thousands or millions if stated (PGC norma 2.ª.4).

## Which model applies

| Model | Who | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Normal (PGC) | Any company above the art. 257 / 258 thresholds, all EIPs | PGC 3rd part, normas 3.ª-4.ª |
| Abreviado (PGC) | Companies within LSC art. 257 (balance, ECPN, memoria) and/or art. 258 (PyG), applying the full PGC | LSC arts. 257-258, 261 |
| PYMES (PGC-PYMES) | Companies within RD 1515/2007 art. 2 that chose PGC-PYMES; models equal the abbreviated ones minus items for operations PGC-PYMES does not regulate | RD 1515/2007 art. 2; PGC-PYMES introduction, 3 |
| Mixed | A company may combine an abbreviated balance with a normal PyG or vice versa when it meets one test but not the other (e.g. normal balance, abbreviated PyG) | LSC arts. 257-258 are separate tests |

Sociedades colectivas and comanditarias simples and empresarios individuales must formulate at least the abbreviated accounts (PGC norma 4.ª.2). Thresholds and the two-year rule: `auditoria-y-umbrales.md`.

## Balance

Three blocks (Cco art. 35.1): **Activo** (A no corriente, B corriente), **Patrimonio neto y pasivo** (A Patrimonio neto: A-1 Fondos propios, A-2 Ajustes por cambios de valor, A-3 Subvenciones, donaciones y legados recibidos; B Pasivo no corriente; C Pasivo corriente). Current means realised or due within the normal operating cycle, generally within 12 months of year-end.

## ECPN and EFE

**ECPN** (Cco art. 35.3) has two parts: (A) *estado de ingresos y gastos reconocidos* (PyG result plus income and expense booked directly in equity through groups 8 and 9, and transfers to PyG) and (B) *estado total de cambios en el patrimonio neto* (all equity movements, including transactions with owners, and adjustments for policy changes and errors under NRV 22).

**EFE** (Cco art. 35.4) shows cash flows from operating, investing and financing activities (indirect method in the PGC model).

Both are optional when the company can formulate an abbreviated balance (LSC art. 257.3; PGC norma 1.ª as amended by RD 602/2016) and voluntary under PGC-PYMES (norma 1.ª). If included voluntarily, they follow the PGC model.

## Memoria

Completes, expands and comments the other documents (LSC art. 259; Cco art. 35.5). Minimum mentions in LSC art. 260; abbreviated filers may omit those set by regulation but always give the items listed in LSC art. 261. The PGC normal memoria headings start: 1 Actividad de la empresa, 2 Bases de presentación (imagen fiel, critical estimates, comparability, policy changes, error corrections), 3 Aplicación de resultados (proposal and limits on dividends), 4 Normas de registro y valoración, 5 Inmovilizado material, 6 Inversiones inmobiliarias, 7 Inmovilizado intangible, 8 Arrendamientos, 9 Instrumentos financieros, then equity, existencias, foreign currency, situación fiscal, income and expenses, provisions, environment, staff, payments based on shares, subsidies, business combinations, joint ventures, held-for-sale, post-balance-sheet events, related parties, other information. The PYMES memoria is shorter (activity, bases, aplicación de resultados, NRV, fixed assets, financial assets and liabilities, fondos propios, situación fiscal, ingresos y gastos, subsidies, related parties, other information).

## Informe de gestión

Faithful account of the business's development and position, main risks and uncertainties, key financial and, where relevant, non-financial indicators, post-closing events, outlook, R&D, own shares (LSC art. 262.1-2). Companies that cannot file an abbreviated PyG state their average supplier payment period and, if above the legal maximum, the measures to reduce it (art. 262.1). Not required for companies that formulate balance and ECPN abbreviated (art. 262.3); they then disclose own-share acquisitions in the memoria. Signed by all administrators (art. 253.2).

## Estado de información no financiera

Required (in the informe de gestión or a separate report with the same content as Cco art. 49.5-7) for companies with more than 250 employees that are either EIPs (other than SMEs under Directive 2013/34) or, for two consecutive years, exceed at least one of: activo 20,000,000 € or cifra de negocios 40,000,000 € (Ley 11/2018 DT, apartado 3, applicable from 2021 onward; the consolidated LSC art. 262.5 still shows the original 500-employee wording). Subsidiaries covered by a parent's consolidated report are exempt (art. 262.5). The EINF must be verified by an independent provider of verification services and approved as a separate agenda item at the junta (Cco art. 49.6). The CSRD has not been transposed: bill 121/000038 lapsed on 6-10-2026.

## Formulation and signatures

- Administrators formulate within 3 months of year-end (LSC art. 253.1; PGC norma 2.ª.2).
- Signed by all administrators; a missing signature is noted with the reason on each document (LSC art. 253.2; Cco art. 37). The formulation date appears before the signatures (Cco art. 37.3).
- Reformulation after formulation and before approval only for risks that significantly affect the true and fair view (Cco art. 38.c); the auditor then issues a new report (LSC art. 270.2).
- Every document identifies its name, the company and the year (PGC norma 2.ª.3).

## Consolidated accounts and NIIF-UE

- A parent of a group (control, Cco art. 42) formulates consolidated accounts and a consolidated informe de gestión under the NOFCAC (RD 1159/2010).
- Exemptions (Cco art. 43): the group does not exceed two of the abbreviated PyG thresholds (11.4 M€ / 22.8 M€ / 250) unless a member is an EIP; sub-group included in an EU parent's consolidation (with conditions); immaterial subsidiaries.
- Groups with securities admitted to trading on an EU regulated market must use IFRS as adopted by the EU (NIIF-UE) in their consolidated accounts; other groups may opt for NIIF-UE for consolidated accounts and must then keep applying it (Cco art. 43 bis). Individual accounts always follow the PGC.
