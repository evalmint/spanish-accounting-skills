## 1. Recognition: asset or expense

An item is an asset when it is a resource controlled by the company from which future economic benefits are expected, and its cost can be measured reliably (PGC Marco Conceptual, 4.º and 5.º). Fixed assets are those used in the activity for more than one year.

| Purchase | Treatment | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Machine, vehicle, furniture, computer used over years | Asset | NRV 2.ª |
| Repair or maintenance that keeps the asset working | Expense **622** | NRV 3.ª f a contrario |
| Renovation, extension or improvement that increases capacity, productivity or useful life | Add to the asset; derecognise the replaced part | NRV 3.ª f |
| Major overhaul (gran reparación) expected periodically | Identified as a separate component at acquisition and amortised until the overhaul | NRV 3.ª g |
| Tools not part of a machine, used under a year | Expense | NRV 3.ª d |
| Tools used over a year | Asset (**214** Utillaje), with an annual count and write-down recommended | NRV 3.ª d |
| Moulds used permanently in series production | Asset | NRV 3.ª d |
| Improvements to rented premises that cannot be separated (operating lease) | Asset, amortised over the shorter of the lease term (incl. likely renewals) and economic life | NRV 3.ª h |
| Research | Expense, or capitalise in **200** if the NRV 6.ª conditions are met (specific project, cost clearly assigned, reasonable technical and commercial success) | NRV 6.ª a |
| Development | Capitalise in **201** when the same conditions are met | NRV 6.ª a |
| Software bought or developed in-house for own use, including website development costs | **206** Aplicaciones informáticas | NRV 6.ª e |
| Set-up costs (gastos de establecimiento), training, advertising, internally generated brands, mastheads, customer lists | Expense: never an intangible asset when generated internally | NRV 5.ª |

There is no monetary threshold in the PGC. Immaterial items may be expensed under the principle of materiality (PGC Marco Conceptual, principio de importancia relativa); the company should fix a written internal policy and apply it consistently.

## 2. Cost

Cost is the acquisition price or production cost (NRV 2.ª 1):

| Included | Not included |
|---|---|
| Invoice price after discounts | Recoverable IVA (it goes to **472**) |
| Transport, customs duties, insurance until in place, installation, assembly, site preparation | Training of staff, start-up losses |
| Non-recoverable IVA (e.g. the 50 % non-deductible on a car, or IVA under a prorrata) | Interest on financing, unless the asset needs over one year to get ready (then interest before entry into operation is capitalised) |
| Present value of dismantling or site restoration obligations, against **143** Provisión por desmantelamiento, retiro o rehabilitación del inmovilizado | Administrative and general overheads |
| For own-built assets: materials, direct costs and a reasonable share of indirect production costs (credited to **731** etc.) | |

Assets received as a non-monetary capital contribution are valued at fair value (NRV 2.ª 1.4). Commercial exchanges (permutas comerciales) at fair value of what is given; non-commercial at book value of what is given (NRV 2.ª 1.3).

**Land and building:** value separately (NRV 3.ª b). For tax, if the land value is not known, split the price using the cadastral values of land and construction in the purchase year, unless another market-based split is proven (RIS art. 3.2).

## 4. Inmovilizado intangible (subgroup 20)

| Account | Content | Book amortisation | Tax |
|---|---|---|---|
| **200** Investigación | Capitalised research | Within 5 years (NRV 6.ª a) | Free amortisation (LIS art. 12.3.c), excluding items with their own free regime |
| **201** Desarrollo | Capitalised development | Useful life, generally within 5 years; longer must be justified (NRV 6.ª a) | Free amortisation (LIS art. 12.3.c) |
| **202** Concesiones administrativas | Administrative concessions | Concession period | Useful life |
| **203** Propiedad industrial | Patents, trade marks registered (acquired or developed) | Useful life | Useful life; if not reliably estimable, max 5 %/year (LIS art. 12.2) |
| **204** Fondo de comercio | Goodwill acquired in a business combination | Useful life; presumed 10 years unless proven otherwise (NRV 6.ª c) | Max 5 %/year (LIS art. 12.2) |
| **205** Derechos de traspaso | Lease rights acquired (traspaso) | Useful life | Useful life |
| **206** Aplicaciones informáticas | Software, including in-house development for own use and website development (NRV 6.ª e) | Useful life | Max 33 % (table) |
| **209** Anticipos para inmovilizaciones intangibles | Advances | Not amortised | |

Since 2016 every intangible has a definite life; when the life cannot be estimated reliably, amortise over 10 years (NRV 5.ª, as amended by RD 602/2016). Tax: intangibles whose life cannot be reliably estimated, and goodwill, are deductible at most at one twentieth (5 %) per year (LIS art. 12.2), creating a temporary difference with the 10-year book life.

## 5. Inversiones inmobiliarias (subgroup 22)

Land or buildings held to earn rent or for capital appreciation, not for use in production, supply, administration or sale in the ordinary course (NRV 4.ª, with the NRV 2.ª–3.ª measurement rules).

| Account | Content |
|---|---|
| **220** Inversiones en terrenos y bienes naturales | Land held as investment |
| **221** Inversiones en construcciones | Buildings rented out |
| **282** Amortización acumulada de las inversiones inmobiliarias | |
| **292** Deterioro de valor de las inversiones inmobiliarias | |

A company whose main asset is a rented property may be an *entidad patrimonial* for LIS art. 5.2, which removes ERD incentives (LIS art. 101.1): flag it.

| Cuenta | Debe | Haber |
|---|---|---|

| **472** Hacienda Pública, IVA soportado | 315.00 | |

| Cuenta | Debe | Haber |
|---|---|---|

## 10. Low-value items

| Question | Answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Accounting threshold | None in the PGC; materiality and consistency | Marco Conceptual |
| Tax: free amortisation of small items | New tangible items with unit value ≤ 300 €, up to 25,000 € per tax period (prorated for shorter periods). Applies to all IS taxpayers | LIS art. 12.3.e |
| Does free amortisation need booking? | No: free and accelerated amortisation are deductible even if not booked in profit and loss (LIS art. 11.3.1.º), so capitalising the item in the books and deducting it in full for tax creates a temporary difference | LIS art. 11.3.1.º |
| Items over 300 € expensed in the books | Allowed for tax only as far as the expense corresponds to effective depreciation; a large item expensed at once can be adjusted by the AEAT | LIS art. 12.1 |
| Autónomos | Same 300 € / 25,000 € rule through LIRPF art. 28 and 30 | LIRPF |

In practice, many small companies expense items under a few hundred euros directly; that is accepted when immaterial. For anything near or above 300 €, capitalise and use the 12.3.e free amortisation for tax.

## 11. Fixed-asset register

No law prescribes a specific *registro de inmovilizado*. The obligation comes from several places:

- The *libro de inventarios y cuentas anuales* must contain the closing inventory each year (Código de Comercio art. 28.1); assets are part of it.
- The memoria must disclose movements of each class: gross cost, accumulated amortisation, impairment, additions, disposals (PGC part 3, memoria models; in the Pymes model it is note 5, *Inmovilizado material, intangible e inversiones inmobiliarias*) (see `spanish-financial-reporting`).
- Tax deductions must be justified (LGT art. 105–106): each amortisation charge needs the asset, its cost, start date and coefficient.
- IVA: companies that must regularise deductions for bienes de inversión keep a *Libro registro de bienes de inversión* (RIVA art. 65; §12).

Minimum data per asset card:

| Field | Why |
|---|---|
| Description, location, serial number | Identification and physical count |
| PGC account and table category | Correct coefficient |
| Supplier, invoice number and date | Proof of cost and IVA |
| Cost, with non-deductible IVA if any | Amortisation base |
| Date of entry into operation | Amortisation starts then (RIS art. 3.3) |
| Book method, useful life, residual value | NRV 2.ª 2.1 |
| Tax regime: table coefficient, libertad, acelerada, art. 106 | Temporary differences |
| Accumulated book and tax amortisation | Two columns, the difference is the temporary difference |
| Impairment | Not deductible when booked |
| New or used | RIS art. 4.3 |
| IVA bien de inversión: deducted IVA, prorrata, regularisation years | RIVA art. 65 |
| Disposal date, proceeds, result | Derecognition and IVA adjustment |

## 12. IVA: bienes de inversión

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | Tangible goods normally used for more than one year as work instruments or means of operation | LIVA art. 108.Uno |
| Excluded | Spare parts for repairs, repair works, packaging, work clothes, and any item with acquisition value below 3,005.06 € (500,000 pesetas) | LIVA art. 108.Dos |
| Regularisation period | Four calendar years after the year of acquisition (or entry into use); nine years for land and buildings | LIVA art. 107.Uno and Tres |
| When regularised | Only if the definitive deduction percentage of a year differs by more than 10 points from the one in the acquisition year | LIVA art. 107.Uno |
| Where | In the last 303 of the year (box 43) | LIVA art. 107.Siete; AEAT 303 instructions |
| Vehicles and goods with mixed use | The deduction percentage of LIVA art. 95.Tres is regularised with the same mechanism | LIVA art. 95.Tres.3.ª |

Regularisation only matters for businesses with a prorrata or a change in deductible activity. A fully taxable business whose percentage stays at 100 % never regularises, but must still check sales during the period (LIVA art. 110; see `leasing-and-disposal.md`).
