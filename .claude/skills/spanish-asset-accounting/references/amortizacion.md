# Amortización: Book, Tax and the Difference

Book amortisation under the PGC, tax amortisation under the LIS, the incentives, and how the two are bridged with deferred tax. Accounts are **PGC 2007**. Tax rules are territorio común (Ley 27/2014, LIS; RD 634/2015, RIS); the forales have their own tables.

## 1. Book amortisation (NRV 2.ª 2.1)

| Rule | Content |
|---|---|
| Basis | Cost minus residual value |
| Pattern | Systematic and rational over useful life, reflecting use and technical or commercial obsolescence; straight-line is the usual choice |
| Components | Each part with significant cost and a different life is amortised separately (e.g. a building's lifts, a lorry's engine overhaul) |
| Start | When the asset is available for use (in condiciones de funcionamiento); land is not amortised |
| Changes in life, residual value or method | Change in estimate, prospective (NRV 22.ª), unless it is an error |
| After impairment | Future amortisation recalculated on the new carrying amount |
| Frequency | Annual at least; monthly booking is common and makes interim results meaningful |

Entry: **681** Amortización del inmovilizado material / **281x** (e.g. **2817**); intangibles **680** / **280**; real estate investments **682** / **282**.

The book life is a management estimate. Using the tax table periods is common and accepted when they reasonably reflect use; it also avoids temporary differences.

## 2. Tax amortisation: the methods of LIS art. 12.1

Amortisation is deductible when it corresponds to effective depreciation. Depreciation is effective when it results from one of these methods (LIS art. 12.1; RIS art. 3–6):

| Method | Rule | Exclusions | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| a) Table | Any linear coefficient between the maximum coefficient and the one implied by the maximum period | | LIS art. 12.1.a; RIS art. 4.1 |
| b) Porcentaje constante (declining balance) | A constant percentage on the pending value: table linear coefficient × 1.5 (period < 5 years), × 2 (5 to < 8 years), × 2.5 (≥ 8 years); never below 11 %; the remainder is deducted in the last year of life | Buildings, furniture and enseres | LIS art. 12.1.b; RIS art. 5 |
| c) Números dígitos (sum of years' digits) | Digits based on the table period | Buildings, furniture and enseres | LIS art. 12.1.c; RIS art. 6 |
| d) Plan approved by the AEAT | Taxpayer's plan accepted by the administration | | LIS art. 12.1.d; RIS art. 7 |
| e) Justified by the taxpayer | The taxpayer proves the actual depreciation | | LIS art. 12.1.e |

Amortisable base: acquisition price or production cost minus residual value; for buildings, excluding land (RIS art. 3.2). Start: tangible assets and real estate investments from entry into operation; intangibles from when they can produce income (RIS art. 3.3). The asset must be amortised within its useful life, which for the table method is the maximum period.

**The booking requirement (LIS art. 11.3.1.º):** an expense is deductible only if booked in profit and loss (or reserves when a rule says so), except free and accelerated amortisation, which may be deducted without booking. If book amortisation in a year is lower than the minimum implied by the maximum period, the shortfall can be recovered later only within the rules of RIS art. 4.1 (excess over the maximum coefficient in later years is attributed to the earlier year, up to the minimum).

## 4. Used assets, shifts, improvements

| Case | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Asset acquired used (not put into operation for the first time) | Amortise on the price paid up to 2 × the amount from the max coefficient; or on the original cost if known (or appraised) with the max coefficient. From a group company: original cost basis, capped at the max coefficient on the price paid if higher. Buildings under 10 years old are not "used" for this rule | RIS art. 4.3 |
| Asset used in more than one normal 8-hour shift | Coefficient = minimum coefficient + (max − min) × hours worked / 8; not for assets that by nature run continuously | RIS art. 4.2 |
| Improvements, extensions, renewals capitalised | Amortised over the remaining life of the main asset, at the asset's effective rate | RIS art. 3.4 |
| Intangible with reliably estimable life | Over that life | LIS art. 12.2 |
| Intangible without reliable life, and goodwill | Max 1/20 (5 %) per year | LIS art. 12.2 |
| Assets used in R&D (except buildings), capitalised R&D | Free amortisation; buildings used in R&D straight-line over 10 years | LIS art. 12.3.b and c |

## 5. Libertad de amortización

| Regime | Who | Scope and limits | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| New tangible items ≤ 300 € | Any IS taxpayer | Up to 25,000 € per tax period (prorated for shorter periods) | LIS art. 12.3.e |
| R&D assets and capitalised R&D | Any | As in §4 | LIS art. 12.3.b, c |
| Sociedades laborales | SAL/SLL | Assets acquired in the first five years after qualification | LIS art. 12.3.a |
| ERD with job creation | Empresa de reducida dimensión | New tangible assets and real estate investments put into use in a year the ERD conditions are met; amount = 120,000 € × increase in average workforce (two decimals) over the 24 months from the start of the period, maintained a further 24 months. Breach: pay the excess tax plus interest | LIS art. 102 |
| Electric and fuel-cell vehicles, charging points | Any | New FCV, FCHV, BEV, REEV, PHEV vehicles (Reglamento General de Vehículos annex II) used in the activity, and new normal or high-power EV charging infrastructure, entering into operation in tax periods starting in 2024, 2025 and 2026 | LIS DA 18.ª (RDL 4/2024; extended to 2026 by RDL 7/2026 art. 37.2) |
| Renewable self-consumption and renewable thermal installations replacing fossil ones | Any | Entering into operation 2023–2026 (works started or finished in the same year); max 500,000 € of investment; average workforce maintained 24 months; buildings excluded; ERDs choose between this and art. 102 | LIS DA 17.ª (RDL 18/2022; 2026 added by RDL 7/2026 art. 37.1) |

The 2026 years in DA 17.ª and 18.ª were added by RDL 7/2026 of 20 March 2026 with effect for periods starting from 1 January 2025 and not concluded at 22 March 2026, after RDL 16/2025 (23 December 2025) and RDL 2/2026 (3 February 2026) had made the same change and been repealed by Congress. A vehicle that started operating in early 2026 before 22 March is covered under the RDL 7/2026 text, but whether an RDL 7/2026 validation or later amendment changes this was not checked (unverified).

Free amortisation does not need to be booked (LIS art. 11.3.1.º); the asset's tax value is reduced by the amount deducted (LIS art. 12.3, last paragraph). The book amortisation continues normally, so a taxable temporary difference arises (§7).

IRPF: autónomos in estimación directa can apply LIS art. 12.3.e and the ERD incentives through LIRPF art. 30.1 (see `spanish-autonomo-tax`).

## 6. Empresas de reducida dimensión

| Rule | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ERD test | Net turnover of the prior tax period < 10 M€ (annualised for short periods; at group level under Código de Comercio art. 42, including family-controlled groups); not an entidad patrimonial | LIS art. 101.1–3 |
| Transition | Incentives continue for three years after exceeding 10 M€, if ERD in that year and the two before | LIS art. 101.4 |
| Amortización acelerada | New tangible assets, real estate investments and intangibles: up to 2 × the max linear coefficient of the table; also own-built and assets under construction contracts. Compatible with other benefits | LIS art. 103.1–4 |
| Intangibles of LIS art. 12.2 (no reliable life, goodwill) acquired while ERD | Deductible at 150 % of the art. 12.2 amount (7.5 %/year) | LIS art. 103.5 (refers to art. 13.3, reading of the consolidated text; the reference is a legacy numbering) |
| Leasing | The art. 106 limit becomes 2 × coefficient × 1.5 (i.e. 3 ×) | LIS art. 106.6 |
| Global bad-debt provision | 1 % of debtors deductible | LIS art. 104 |

**Example:** an ERD buys a new machine for 60,000 € (12 % table). Book life 10 years (6,000/year). Tax: up to 24 % (14,400/year) under art. 103. The extra 8,400 a year is a taxable temporary difference that reverses once the tax value reaches zero (year 5 onward).

## 7. When book and tax differ

The difference between the tax deduction and the book charge is a negative or positive adjustment to the accounting result in modelo 200. Under the full PGC and the Pymes plan (except microempresas applying RD 1515/2007 art. 4.3, 2.ª), a temporary difference creates deferred tax (NRV 13.ª PGC).

| Situation | Adjustment in modelo 200 | Deferred tax | Entry |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tax amortisation > book (libertad, acelerada, art. 106) | Negative now, positive later | Taxable temporary difference: liability | **6301** Impuesto diferido / **479** Pasivos por diferencias temporarias imponibles |
| Book amortisation > tax maximum (life shorter than the table period, goodwill at 10 % vs 5 %) | Positive now, negative later | Deductible temporary difference: asset (if recovery is probable) | **4740** Activos por diferencias temporarias deducibles / **6301** |
| Impairment booked | Positive now, negative when deductible (amortisation or disposal) | Asset | **4740** / **6301** |
| Reversal years | Opposite adjustments | Release | Opposite entries |

Rate: the tax rate expected when the difference reverses (NRV 13.ª). Pymes and small companies with reduced or transitional IS rates must use the rate of the reversal year; see `spanish-corporate-tax` for the rate schedule and `spanish-year-end-closing` for the full calculation.

**Example (taxable difference):** 25 % rate, 10,000 € more tax amortisation than book in the year:

| Cuenta | Debe | Haber |
|---|---|---|
| **6301** Impuesto diferido | 2,500.00 | |
| **479** Pasivos por diferencias temporarias imponibles | | 2,500.00 |

Microempresas that apply the specific criteria book only current tax (no **479**/**4740**) and disclose the differences in the memoria (RD 1515/2007 art. 4.3, 2.ª).

## 8. Deterioro (impairment)

| Step | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Test | At least at year end, look for signs of impairment; if any, estimate the recoverable amount (higher of fair value less costs to sell and value in use), item by item or by cash-generating unit | NRV 2.ª 2.2 |
| Book the loss | **691** Pérdidas por deterioro del inmovilizado material / **291** (intangible **690**/**290**, real estate **692**/**292**) | |
| Reversal | **291** / **791**, up to the carrying amount without the impairment | NRV 2.ª 2.2 |
| Goodwill | Impairment of goodwill is never reversed | NRV 6.ª c |
| Tax | Not deductible when booked (LIS art. 13.2.a); deducted later through tax amortisation over the remaining life, or on disposal or derecognition (LIS art. 20) | LIS art. 13.2, 20 |

After an impairment, book amortisation is recalculated on the new carrying amount; tax amortisation continues on the tax value: track both.

## 9. Worked examples

**A. Office furniture 12,000 €, entry into use 1 April, book life 10 years, tax 10 % (furniture table: max 10 %, max period 20 years).**

Year 1 book charge: 12,000 × 10 % × 9/12 = 900.

| Cuenta | Debe | Haber |
|---|---|---|
| **681** Amortización del inmovilizado material | 900.00 | |
| **2816** Amortización acumulada de mobiliario | | 900.00 |

Book = tax: no difference.

**B. Laptop 280 €, new, in a company that uses LIS art. 12.3.e.** Book: capitalised in **217**, amortised over 4 years (70/year). Tax: 280 deducted in year 1. Adjustment year 1: −210; years 2–4: +70 each. Deferred tax liability created and released accordingly. (Many companies instead expense such items directly; then book = tax and no adjustment.)

**C. Electric car (BEV) 40,000 €, IVA deductible 50 %, entered into use in May 2025.** Cost booked = 40,000 + 4,200 non-deductible IVA = 44,200. Book life 8 years. Tax: free amortisation under LIS DA 18.ª: deduct 44,200 in 2025. Book charge 2025 (8 months): 3,683.33. Adjustment 2025: −40,516.67; in later years the book amortisation is added back (+5,525/year).

## 10. Ask the user

- Date each asset entered into operation (not the invoice date).
- New or used? Bought from a group company?
- Is the company an ERD this year (prior-year turnover, group)?
- Which book life does the company use for each class? Is there a written policy?
- For an EV or renewable installation: type and entry-into-operation date; workforce figures for DA 17.ª.
- Does the company apply PGC Pymes microempresa criteria (no deferred tax)?
