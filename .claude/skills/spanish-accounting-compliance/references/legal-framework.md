## PGC, PGC-PYMES and microempresas

**PGC (RD 1514/2007).** Mandatory for all empresarios, any legal form, except those that may and choose to apply PGC-PYMES (art. 2). Five parts: 1 Marco conceptual, 2 Normas de registro y valoración (NRV 1.ª-23.ª), 3 Cuentas anuales (normas de elaboración, models, memoria), 4 Cuadro de cuentas, 5 Definiciones y relaciones contables. Parts 4 and 5 are not binding except where they contain recognition or valuation criteria (art. 2, second paragraph).

**PGC-PYMES (RD 1515/2007).** Optional for any entity that, for two consecutive year-ends, meets two of: total activo ≤ 4,000,000 €, importe neto de la cifra de negocios ≤ 8,000,000 €, average headcount ≤ 50 (art. 2.1, as amended by RD 602/2016 with effect from 1-1-2016). In the first year, the test is at the first year-end. Group companies aggregate the group's figures. Never available (art. 2.2) to: EIPs (Ley 22/2015 art. 3.5), members of a group that formulates or should formulate consolidated accounts, entities with a non-euro functional currency, and financial entities taking funds from the public. Once chosen, applied in full (art. 3.1) and kept for at least three years (art. 2.3). Gaps are filled from the PGC, except the rules on non-current assets held for sale (art. 3.2). PGC-PYMES cuentas anuales are balance, PyG and memoria; ECPN and EFE are voluntary (PGC-PYMES 3rd part, norma 1.ª).

**Criterios específicos para microempresas (RD 1515/2007 art. 4).** Available to PGC-PYMES users that for two consecutive year-ends meet two of: activo ≤ 1,000,000 €, cifra de negocios ≤ 2,000,000 €, headcount ≤ 10. For the activo test, add the outstanding finance-lease commitments. Applied jointly and kept for at least three years:

| Topic | Micro treatment |
|---|---|
| Finance leases (not of land or other non-depreciable assets) | Instalments expensed as accrued; asset recognised only when the purchase option is exercised, at the option price; detailed memoria table |
| Corporate income tax | Expense = the tax payable per the year's return; no deferred tax (adjust instalments at year-end against a payable or receivable) |

Leaving the micro criteria triggers retroactive application with adjustment against reserves and a memoria note (RD 1515/2007 DA 1.ª).

## Who applies what

| Entity | Framework | Books |
|---|---|---|
| SL / SA within PGC-PYMES limits | PGC-PYMES (option), micro criteria (option) | Cco books + actas + socios/acciones + socio único contracts |
| SL / SA above limits, EIP, groups | PGC (normal or abbreviated models by size) | Same |
| Sociedad colectiva / comanditaria simple | PGC or PGC-PYMES; abbreviated models allowed (PGC 3rd part, norma 4.ª.2) | Cco books + actas |
| Autónomo, mercantile activity, estimación directa normal | Cco accounting (PGC or PGC-PYMES) | Diario, Inventarios; also tax books if a professional |
| Autónomo, simplificada or non-mercantile, or professional | No Cco accounting required | Libros registro (RIRPF art. 68.3-5) |
| Autónomo in estimación objetiva (módulos) | None | Keep invoices; libro de bienes de inversión if amortising |
| Non-profit entities | Sector adaptation (RD 1491/2011), may use PGC-PYMES content (RD 1515/2007 art. 5) | Per their own rules |

## Territorial scope

The Cco, LSC, PGC, ICAC rules and the Registro Mercantil apply throughout Spain. Tax rules differ: Álava, Gipuzkoa, Bizkaia and Navarra have their own *Impuesto sobre Sociedades* and IRPF and their own invoicing systems (TicketBAI, Batuz in Bizkaia), and Verifactu does not apply there. Canarias applies IGIC (with its own libros registro); Ceuta and Melilla apply IPSI. Always confirm the tax domicile before applying tax-book rules.
