## In force

### Impuesto sobre Sociedades rates for small companies (Ley 7/2024)

Ley 7/2024 (BOE 21-12-2024) rewrote LIS art. 29.1 with effect for periods starting on or after 1-01-2025 and added a transitional schedule in LIS DT 44.ª. *Microempresa* here means cifra de negocios of the previous period below 1 M€ (LIS art. 29.1, measured under art. 101.2-3); *entidad de reducida dimensión* (ERD) means cifra below 10 M€ (LIS art. 101). Not for *entidades patrimoniales*.

| Period starting in | Cifra < 1 M€: first 50,000 € of base | Cifra < 1 M€: rest | ERD (cifra < 10 M€) | General |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2023-2024 (old art. 29.1, Ley 31/2022, unverified) | 23 % | 23 % | 25 % | 25 % |
| 2025 | 21 % | 22 % | 24 % | 25 % |
| 2026 | 19 % | 21 % | 23 % | 25 % |
| 2027 | 17 % | 20 % | 22 % | 25 % |
| 2028 | 17 % | 20 % | 21 % | 25 % |
| 2029 onward | 17 % | 20 % | 20 % | 25 % |

New companies with economic activity keep 15 % in the first period with a positive base and the next one (LIS art. 29.1). Use the rate of the period's start date when booking the IS expense at year-end (`spanish-year-end-closing`).

### Reserva de capitalización (LIS art. 25, Ley 7/2024)

From periods starting on or after 1-01-2025: reduction of 20 % of the increase in equity (was 10 %), rising to 23 %, 26.5 % or 30 % if average headcount grows 2-5 %, 5-10 % or over 10 % and is held 3 years. Limit 20 % of the base before the reduction, or 25 % if cifra de negocios < 1 M€. The equity increase and the non-distributable reserve are kept 3 years (was 5); DT 43.ª applies the 3 years to reserves whose period had not expired at the start of the first period from 1-01-2024. Accounting: a separate, non-distributable reserve with an appropriate title (art. 25.1.b), booked when the profit is applied (`spanish-year-end-closing`).

### B2B electronic invoice (Ley 18/2022 Crea y Crece, RD 238/2026)

RD 238/2026 (BOE 31-03-2026, in force 20-04-2026) regulates mandatory B2B e-invoicing and amends the Reglamento de facturación. Orden HAC/1028/2026 (BOE 5-10-2026, in force 6-10-2026, per the AEAT sede) regulates the public e-invoicing solution and starts the clocks: businesses with volumen de operaciones over 8 M€ must apply it from 6-10-2027 (12 months), the rest from 6-10-2028 (24 months). Detail: `spanish-e-invoicing`.

### Loss moratorium for dissolution (RDL 7/2026 art. 30)

For the dissolution cause of LSC art. 363.1.e (equity below half of capital), losses of 2020 and 2021 are ignored until the close of the year starting in 2026. If losses of 2022-2026 alone bring equity below half of capital, the administrators must call a junta within two months of year-end (LSC art. 365) unless capital is increased or reduced. Administrators who had already formulated the 2025 accounts could reformulate them within one month of 22-03-2026, with the junta within three months of the new formulation. RDL 4/2025 had recovered the same moratorium earlier, with a similar reformulation window for the 2024 accounts.

## Bills that lapsed in October 2026

Both bills were declared *caducado* with the end of their procedure on 6-10-2026 (BOCG D-610, 4-11-2026). Nothing in them is law.

| Bill | What it proposed |
|---|---|
| 121/000075, *criterios de tamaño de las empresas a efectos de información financiera* (BOCG A-75-1, 21-11-2025) | Transpose Delegated Directive 2023/2775: abbreviated balance and PGC-PYMES at activo 7.5 M€ / cifra 15 M€ / 50 employees; audit exemption at 3.565 M€ / 7.125 M€ / 50; medium entity 25 M€ / 50 M€ |
| 121/000038, *Ley de información empresarial sobre sostenibilidad* (BOCG A-38-1, 15-11-2024) | Transpose the CSRD, replace the EINF regime |

Consequence: the LSC and PGC-PYMES thresholds of 4 / 8 / 50, 11.4 / 22.8 / 250 and audit 2.85 / 5.7 / 50 still apply, and the EINF of Ley 11/2018 is still the non-financial reporting regime. Spain is past the 24-12-2024 transposition deadline of Directive 2023/2775. A new bill would need to restart; check the BOE before applying any new threshold.

## What did not change

- Cco arts. 25-49 (last consolidated update 09-05-2023).
- Legalisation: 4 months, telematic (Ley 14/2013 art. 18).
- Formulation 3 months, junta 6 months, deposit 1 month after approval (LSC arts. 253, 164, 279).
- Reserva legal 10 % up to 20 % of capital (LSC art. 274).
- PGC and PGC-PYMES recognition and valuation rules; no new ICAC resolución in 2025-2026 found.
