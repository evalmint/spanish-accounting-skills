## 2. Censal declaration (modelo 036): IAE, IVA, ROI, retentions

Modelo 036 is the only censal form since modelo 037 was abolished from 3 February 2025 (Orden HAC/1526/2024). It records (RGAT art. 9.3):

- **Activity and IAE epígrafe.** A company is exempt from IAE in its first two tax periods (LRHL art. 82.1.b) and afterwards while its net turnover is under 1 M euros (art. 82.1.c). It still declares the activity in modelo 036; modelo 840 is only for those who pay.
- **IVA.** An SL is in the *régimen general*: modelo 303 quarterly unless turnover exceeds 6,010,121.04 euros or it joins REDEME (see `spanish-vat`). State whether purchases start before sales (RGAT art. 9.3.d) to deduct pre-activity IVA.
- **ROI** (*Registro de operadores intracomunitarios*, RGAT art. 9.3.g). Needed to buy or sell goods or services B2B with EU businesses using a Spanish VAT number visible in VIES. Without it, an EU supplier will charge its own VAT and EU customers cannot apply the reverse charge. Ask the founder whether there will be EU clients or suppliers (cloud tools billed from Ireland count).
- **Retentions.** Tick the obligations that apply: employee or professional withholding (modelo 111), rent of business premises (modelo 115).

## 3. Digital certificate and electronic notifications

- An SL must deal with the administration electronically (Ley 39/2015 art. 14.2) and is in the AEAT's compulsory electronic notifications system (RD 1363/2010). Notifications count as delivered after 10 calendar days unopened, so set up alerts (DEHú).
- The usual certificate is the FNMT *certificado de representante de persona jurídica*, requested by the administrator; it generally requires the company to be registered with the administrator's appointment on file (unverified for current FNMT requirements; check sede.fnmt.gob.es). Until then the administrator can act with a personal certificate and a representation.

## 4. Bank, invoicing software and Verifactu

- Move the capital account into the company's name after registration.
- Invoicing software must meet the *Reglamento de sistemas informáticos de facturación* (RD 1007/2023). Dates after RDL 15/2025 (BOE 3 December 2025, convalidated 11 December 2025): **1 January 2027** for IS taxpayers (every SL) and **1 July 2027** for the rest. The earlier dates (1 January and 1 July 2026, set by RD 254/2025) no longer apply. Choose compliant software from day one; see `spanish-e-invoicing`.
- B2B e-invoicing: RD 238/2026 (BOE 31 March 2026) develops the Ley 18/2022 obligation. According to secondary sources the implementing order was published 5 October 2026, starting the clock: companies above 8 M euros turnover from 6 October 2027, the rest from 6 October 2028 (unverified against BOE). See `spanish-e-invoicing`.

## 5. RETA for the administrator (autónomo societario)

**Who.** An administrator who manages and has effective control (LGSS art. 305.2.b; presumptions in `capital-and-statutes.md` §5). Work it out from the shares and roles and say it.

**Contributions 2026** (Orden PJC/297/2026, BOE 31 March 2026, art. 18):
- Contribution bases follow monthly net income in 15 tramos; the tables for 2026 are the same as 2025's (reduced table from 653.59 euros, general table from 950.98 to 1,928.10 euros minimum, maximum 5,101.20 euros).
- Rates: contingencias comunes 28.30 %, profesionales 1.30 %, MEI 0.90 %; with cese de actividad and formación the total is commonly given as 31.50 % (components other than CC, CP and MEI unverified in the Orden text).
- **Societarios' floor:** the definitive base of a 305.2.b or e autónomo cannot be below the rule in LGSS art. 308.1.a regla 4ª, which secondary sources read as the Régimen General group 7 minimum, **1,424.40 euros a month in 2026**; they may keep their 2025 provisional base (often 1,000 euros) during 2026, with the floor applied at the annual regularisation (Orden PJC/297/2026 art. 18.4). This is a 2026 change from the flat 1,000-euro floor of 2023-2025; confirm with the Seguridad Social or a graduado social before quoting a figure.
- Net income for societarios is computed with a 3 % generic expense deduction instead of 7 % (secondary sources, unverified).

**Tarifa plana.** 80 euros a month for the first 12 months of a first alta, extendable 12 months if income stays under the SMI (Ley 20/2007 art. 38 ter). Whether a societario qualifies has been disputed; secondary sources say the Seguridad Social now accepts it (unverified). Not available to someone in RETA in the previous two years (three if they used it before) (unverified).

**Startup founders in pluriactividad** (also employed elsewhere): 100 % bonus on the minimum-base contribution for up to three years (Ley 20/2007 art. 38 quinquies, added by Ley 28/2022). See `startup-law.md`.

## 6. First employee

Before the first hire: employer registration and *código de cuenta de cotización*, contract communicated through Contrat@, alta in the Seguridad Social before the start, withholding (modelo 111) and the occupational risk prevention set-up. Details in `spanish-payroll`.

## 7. Books and legalización

- Compulsory: *Libro Diario* and *Libro de Inventarios y Cuentas Anuales* (Código de Comercio art. 25); *libro de actas* (art. 26); *libro registro de socios* (LSC art. 104); and for an SLU the *libro registro de contratos* with the single partner (LSC art. 16).
- All are kept electronically and legalised telematically at the Registro Mercantil within **4 months of the year-end** (Ley 14/2013 art. 18).
- Annual accounts: prepared within 3 months of year-end, approved by the general meeting within 6 months, filed within one month of approval. See `spanish-accounting-compliance` and `spanish-year-end-closing`.

| Modelo 200 IS | Year | 25 days after the 6 months following year-end (1-25 July for calendar years) | Filed even with no activity |

## 9. Ask the founder

- Will you sell to or buy from EU businesses? (ROI)
- Will anyone be paid a salary, or will you rent premises? (111, 115)
- Expected turnover in the first year? (IVA period, IAE exemption, IS rate)
- Do you, as administrator, already pay into RETA or the Régimen General? (pluriactividad, tarifa plana)
- Which invoicing tool will you use? (Verifactu)
