## 1. The order of things

| # | Step | Who acts | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Name certificate | Founder (online at the Registro Mercantil Central, or the notary/PAE) | *Certificación negativa de denominación* |
| 2 | Bank account "en constitución" and deposit | Founder and bank | *Certificado de depósito* (or skip it, see §3) |
| 3 | Estatutos and escritura | Notary; all founders sign | *Escritura de constitución* |
| 4 | NIF provisional | Notary (usually) or founder, modelo 036 | NIF provisional |
| 5 | ITP-AJD | Notary/gestoría files the exempt self-assessment | Proof of exemption |
| 6 | Inscripción | Notary sends electronically to the *Registro Mercantil Provincial* | Inscribed company, legal personality |
| 7 | NIF definitivo | Founder/gestoría, modelo 036 | Definitive NIF |
| 8 | Titularidad real | Administrators at the Registro Mercantil | Declaration on file |

Then the first-month obligations in `first-month-obligations.md` (censal 036 alta, RETA, ROI, books). Before step 6 the company exists as a *sociedad en formación*: the founders and administrators who act for it are jointly liable for what they sign (LSC arts. 33-36), so keep pre-registration contracts to what is necessary.

## 2. Step 1: certificación negativa de denominación

- Requested from the *Registro Mercantil Central* (RMC), online at rmc.es, by the founder, the notary or a PAE. Up to five names in order of preference.
- The name must not be identical to an existing one; the escritura must use exactly the name on the certificate.
- **Reservation:** 6 months from issue (RRM art. 412.1); extended by 2 months while the escritura is pending registration (per DGSJFP resolution of August 2026, secondary source).
- **Validity for signing:** the certificate is valid for 3 months from issue to sign the escritura (RRM art. 414.1). If it lapses, request a new one with the same name.
- Through the estatutos-tipo telematic route the RMC must answer within 6 business hours (Ley 14/2013 art. 15.3.b).
- Fee: a small registry fee (around 15-25 euros) (unverified, check rmc.es).

Tip for the founder: avoid purely generic names and names that could be confused with a known brand; a trademark search (OEPM) is a separate question from the registry name.

## 3. Step 2: bank account and capital

- **Rule:** cash contributions must be proved before the notary with a certificate of deposit in the company's name (LSC art. 62.1). The certificate is valid **2 months** from its date (art. 62.3).
- **SL alternative:** the founders may skip the certificate by declaring in the escritura that they are **jointly liable** (*responderán solidariamente*) to the company and its creditors for the reality of the contributions (LSC art. 62.2). Useful with 1-euro or very small capital, or when banks are slow.
- After registration the bank releases the funds to the company.

## 4. Step 3: estatutos and escritura before the notary

- The escritura contains the founders' identity, the contributions and participations, the estatutos, the first administrators and the domicile (LSC arts. 20-23). Every founder signs (in person or by power of attorney; an electronic videoconference signature is possible for SL with cash contributions since Ley 11/2023 (unverified for current scope)).
- **Estatutos tipo** (standard articles in RD 421/2015, Orden JUS/1840/2015) speed up the telematic route but fix choices: see `capital-and-statutes.md` §3.
- The founder brings: the name certificate, the deposit certificate (or the joint liability choice), and the identity documents. Never collect identity numbers in the chat.

## 5. Step 4: NIF provisional

- An SL needs a NIF before acting. The notary usually requests it electronically when signing; otherwise the founder files modelo 036.
- The AEAT assigns it within 10 days (RGAT art. 24.1). It is **provisional** until the escritura, estatutos and registration certificate are provided (art. 24.2).
- Modelo 037 no longer exists: it was abolished from 3 February 2025 by Orden HAC/1526/2024; everything goes on modelo 036.

## 6. Step 5: ITP-AJD

- Constitution of companies is exempt in the *operaciones societarias* modality (TRLITPAJD art. 45.I.B.11, as worded by RDL 13/2010 from 3 December 2010). The exempt self-assessment (modelo 600 or the regional equivalent) is normally filed by the notary or gestoría; check who does it.
- Being subject to OS (even if exempt), the escritura does not pay AJD *cuota gradual* (unverified wording, TRLITPAJD art. 31.2).

## 7. Step 6: inscripción in the Registro Mercantil

- Filing deadline: **2 months** from the escritura (LSC art. 32.1); founders and administrators are jointly liable for damage from a late filing.
- The company acquires legal personality with registration (LSC art. 33).
- Times: with estatutos tipo through CIRCE, the registrar must qualify and register within **6 business hours** (Ley 14/2013 art. 15.5.a); without estatutos tipo, a 6-hour initial registration and definitive registration within **5 days** (art. 16.3-16.4) for telematic filings. Paper filings follow the general registry deadline of 15 business days (Código de Comercio art. 18.4) (unverified).
- Publication in the BORME is exempt from fees on the estatutos-tipo route (art. 15.9).

## 8. Step 7: NIF definitivo

File modelo 036 (*modificación*) with the registered escritura within **1 month** of registration (RGAT art. 24.3). If six months pass from the provisional NIF without it, the AEAT may require the documents within 10 days and ultimately revoke the NIF (art. 24.3). On the CIRCE route the definitive NIF is usually communicated automatically.

## 9. Step 8: titularidad real

- *Titular real* (beneficial owner): any natural person owning or controlling, directly or indirectly, **more than 25 %** of capital or votes, or otherwise controlling; failing that, the administrators (Ley 10/2010 art. 4).
- The notary identifies the beneficial owners at the escritura (anti-money-laundering duty).
- Since RD 609/2023 (in force 19 September 2023, *Registro Central de Titularidades Reales*), a newly formed company must file a first declaration **within 1 month of constitution** at the Registro Mercantil; changes later within 10 days (secondary sources; verify the counting rule with the registry). The annual accounts filing also carries the declaration.
- You can work out the owners from the shares; ask only about family links and other control.

## 10. The telematic route: CIRCE, PAE and DUE

- **CIRCE** (Centro de Información y Red de Creación de Empresas) links the notary, registry, AEAT and Seguridad Social. The founder starts at a **PAE** (*Punto de Atención al Emprendedor*), physical or online at paeelectronico.es, which fills the **DUE** (*Documento Único Electrónico*) (Ley 14/2013 art. 13).
- The DUE carries the NIF request, the censal declaration and the RETA alta, so the founder does not repeat them.
- With **estatutos tipo**: name certificate within 6 business hours, notary appointment within 12 business hours, registry within 6 business hours (Ley 14/2013 art. 15). Real timings depend on the bank or the joint liability choice and on the founders' availability.
- Without estatutos tipo: same channel, slower registry (art. 16).
- Classic route: the founder picks a notary directly; the notary still files electronically.

## 12. What the founder checks before signing

- Name matches the certificate exactly; certificate under 3 months old.
- Deposit certificate under 2 months old, or the joint liability clause is in.
- Objeto social covers every activity planned (adding one later needs a statutory change).
- Administration type and whether the post is paid (LSC art. 217).
- Domicile in *territorio común* or not (tax administration).
- Fiscal year: calendar year unless there is a reason.
- If one partner: "Sociedad Limitada Unipersonal" stated and registered (LSC art. 13).
