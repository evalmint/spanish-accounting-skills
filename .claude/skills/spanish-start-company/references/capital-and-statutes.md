## 1. SL or autónomo

There is no right answer in law; give the facts and send the decision to a gestoría or adviser.

| Point | Autónomo (sole trader) | SL |
|---|---|---|
| Liability | Personal and unlimited | Limited to capital (with the < 3,000 euro rule below and administrator liability) |
| Set-up | Censal 036 and RETA alta; no notary | Notary, registry, NIF; days and some hundreds of euros |
| Income tax | IRPF on profit, progressive (see `spanish-autonomo-tax`) | IS on profit: 25 % general, 2026: 19 % on the first 50,000 euros and 21 % above if the prior-year turnover was under 1 M euros; 23 % for *empresas de reducida dimensión*; 15 % for *entidades de nueva creación* in the first profitable year and the next (LIS art. 29.1; DT 44ª). Then IRPF on salary or dividends |
| Social security | RETA on real net income (tramos) | Administrator with control: RETA as *autónomo societario*, with a higher minimum base (see `first-month-obligations.md` §5) |
| Bookkeeping | Libros registro (IRPF); simple | Full PGC or PGC PYMES accounting, annual accounts filed at the Registro Mercantil, IS return |
| Closing down | Censal baja | Dissolution and liquidation, notary and registry |

IS rate path in law (LIS art. 29.1 and DT 44ª, as amended by Ley 7/2024):

| Periods starting in | Turnover < 1 M euros (first 50,000 / rest) | *Reducida dimensión* (art. 101, < 10 M) | General |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | 23 % / 23 % | 25 % | 25 % |
| 2025 | 21 % / 22 % | 24 % | 25 % |
| 2026 | 19 % / 21 % | 23 % | 25 % |
| 2027 | 17 % / 20 % | 22 % | 25 % |
| 2028 | 17 % / 20 % | 21 % | 25 % |
| 2029 on | 17 % / 20 % | 20 % | 25 % |

The 15 % *nueva creación* rate applies unless a lower one applies; none of the reduced rates apply to an *entidad patrimonial* (LIS art. 29.1). A company that takes over an activity a partner with more than 50 % ran in the previous year is not "new" for the 15 % (same article).

## 2. Capital social

**Minimum.** 1 euro for an SL since Ley 18/2022 (in force 19 October 2022), which also abolished the *sociedad en régimen de formación sucesiva* (LSC art. 4 bis, now suppressed).

**Strings while capital is below 3,000 euros** (LSC art. 4.2):
- At least **20 % of the profit** goes to the *reserva legal* until reserve plus capital reach 3,000 euros (instead of the normal 10 % until the reserve is 20 % of capital, LSC art. 274).
- In a liquidation where assets do not cover debts, the partners are **jointly liable** for the difference between 3,000 euros and the subscribed capital.

Tip: 3,000 euros avoids both strings and looks more serious to banks and suppliers; 1 euro is fine for a test or a holding vehicle, if the founder understands the liability rule.

**Dissolution trigger.** Losses that bring equity below half the capital force a capital increase or reduction or dissolution (LSC art. 363.1.e). With tiny capital this triggers early. *Empresas emergentes* get a 3-year grace (Ley 28/2022 art. 13).

**Cash contributions.** Deposit certificate valid 2 months, or the founders' joint liability declaration (LSC art. 62). **Non-cash contributions** (*aportaciones no dinerarias*: a laptop, software, an existing business) need description and valuation in the escritura and carry liability for their value; send these to the notary or a lawyer.

**Participations.** Capital is divided into *participaciones sociales* with nominal value; choose a count that splits cleanly (two equal founders, 3,000 euros: 3,000 participations of 1 euro, 1,500 each). Transfers are restricted by law and statutes (LSC arts. 106-112).

## 3. Estatutos tipo or a medida

| | Estatutos tipo (RD 421/2015) | Estatutos a medida |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | Fastest: 6-hour registry on CIRCE (Ley 14/2013 art. 15) | 6-hour initial and 5-day definitive registration telematically (art. 16) |
| Cost | Reduced aranceles (60 + 40 euros confirmed for *empresas emergentes*, Ley 28/2022 art. 12) | Normal aranceles |
| Content | Fixed options: administration type, unpaid administrator by default, standard transfer rules | Anything lawful: paid administrators, drag/tag along, classes of participations, vesting-related transfer clauses |
| Fit | One or two founders, simple deal | Investors, several founders, paid administrator |

The estatutos must state at least: name, objeto social, domicile, capital and participations, administration and how it works, and the meeting rules (LSC art. 23). A *pacto de socios* (shareholders' agreement) is a private contract next to the estatutos; it binds the parties but generally not the company or third parties.

## 4. Objeto social and CNAE

- Describe the activities concretely (LSC art. 23.b). Generic catch-alls are refused by registrars in practice. Since 2015 the main activity is identified with its CNAE code in the escritura (unverified for current registry practice; ask the notary).
- The IAE epígrafe (tax activity code) is chosen separately in modelo 036, and the CNAE also feeds the work-accident premium in the Seguridad Social.
- Regulated activities (finance, health, security, construction classification) need licences on top.

## 5. Administración

Four forms (LSC art. 210): *administrador único*; several *solidarios* (each can act alone); several *mancomunados* (act jointly; two or more must sign); *consejo de administración* (board, at least three members). An SL may let the general meeting switch between forms written into the estatutos without amending them (art. 210.3).

- Administrators can be natural or legal persons and need not be partners unless the estatutos say so (art. 212). A legal person administrator appoints one natural person (art. 212 bis).
- Barred: unemancipated minors, persons disqualified after insolvency, and persons convicted of the listed crimes (art. 213).
- **Pay:** the post is unpaid unless the estatutos set a remuneration system (art. 217). If the founder-administrator will draw a salary for management, decide this before signing.
- **Social security:** an administrator who manages and has effective control is in RETA (LGSS art. 305.2.b). Control is assumed with at least 50 % of capital, and presumed with half the capital held by close family living together, with one third, or with one quarter plus management functions. Without control, a paid managing administrator is in the Régimen General as an *asimilado* without unemployment cover (LGSS art. 136.2.c). An unpaid administrator without control is not covered by either regime.

## 6. Sociedad unipersonal (SLU)

- The single-partner status and the partner's identity are registered (LSC art. 13.1) and the company states "unipersonal" on documents, invoices and correspondence (art. 13.2).
- If a company becomes single-partner later and does not register it within **6 months**, the single partner becomes personally, unlimitedly and jointly liable for debts of that period (art. 14).
- Contracts between the single partner and the company must be written and entered in a legalised *libro-registro* and mentioned in the notes to the accounts (art. 16).
- The single partner's decisions are minuted and signed (art. 15).

## 7. After registration: capital in the books

The first entries (detail in `spanish-daily-bookkeeping`): capital social **100** against the bank **572**. In an SL participations must be fully paid up at constitution (LSC art. 78), so the "capital pending" accounts are not used. Costs of issuing the equity (notary, registry, legal fees for the escritura) go directly against reserves, not to profit and loss (PGC NRV 9, section on own equity instruments); in practice a debit to **113** net of the tax effect. Other start-up spending (website, advisers not tied to the issue) is an ordinary expense. Confirm the split with `spanish-accounting-compliance`.
