## 1. Who is an empresa emergente

All of these (Ley 28/2022 art. 3.1):

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| New | Newly created, or not more than **5 years** from registration of the escritura; **7 years** for biotech, energy, industrial and other strategic sectors or own technology designed in Spain |
| Not a spin-out of an old business | Not the result of a merger, split or transformation of companies that are not emergentes |
| No dividends | Has not distributed and does not distribute dividends |
| Not listed | Not listed on a regulated market |
| Spanish base | Registered office, domicile or permanent establishment in Spain |
| Staff | **60 %** of the workforce with an employment contract in Spain |
| Innovative | An innovative, scalable business model, assessed by ENISA (art. 4) |

The status ends, among other cases, when annual turnover exceeds **10 M euros**, when an entity that is not an emergente acquires it, or at the end of the 5/7 years (art. 6). In a group, the group or each company must meet the requirements. Founders or directors with tax or social security debts or certain criminal convictions are barred (art. 3.3).

## 2. Certification by ENISA and registration

- ENISA (Empresa Nacional de Innovación) assesses the requirements and the innovative, scalable character; criteria include public funding received, R&D+i spending, market, stage, scalability, team (art. 4).
- ENISA answers within **3 months** of a complete application; silence means approval (art. 4).
- The status is then **registered in the Registro Mercantil**; registration is "necessary and sufficient" to use the law, while the AEAT may later check the requirements for tax benefits (art. 5.1).
- Apply after constitution; an SL can be founded first and certified later within the 5/7 years.

## 3. Corporate tax benefits

| Benefit | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Reduced rate | **15 %** in the first period with positive base and the next three, while the status lasts | Ley 28/2022 art. 7 |
| Deferral of IS | Payment of the IS of the first two periods with positive base may be deferred **12 and 6 months**, without guarantees | art. 8.1 |
| No pagos fraccionados | No IS (or IRNR) instalments while the status lasts | art. 8.2 |
| Losses | No dissolution for losses below half the capital until 3 years after constitution, unless insolvency applies | art. 13 |

Compare with the ordinary *entidad de nueva creación* rate of 15 % for two years (LIS art. 29.1) and the 2026 micro-company rates of 19/21 % (LIS DT 44ª). Only one rate applies; take the most favourable that the company qualifies for, and check with `spanish-corporate-tax`.

## 4. Company-law and registry benefits

- Registration of emergentes and their corporate acts within **5 business days**; with estatutos tipo, **6 business hours** from telematic receipt (art. 11).
- Notary and registry aranceles of **60 and 40 euros** with estatutos tipo, CIRCE and capital under 3,100 euros; BORME publication free of fees (art. 12).
- Foreign individual investors may obtain a **NIF without a NIE**; the AEAT decides within 10 business days and may revoke it if the investment is not proved within six months (art. 9).

## 5. Stock options and employee equity

LIRPF changes in force from 1 January 2023 (Ley 28/2022 DF 3ª):

- **Exemption** of shares or participations delivered to employees of an emergente up to **50,000 euros a year** (12,000 euros for other companies), including shares from exercising options. The offer need not be made to all employees on equal terms; it must fit the company's general pay policy. For options, the startup requirements are tested when the option is **granted** (LIRPF art. 42.3.f). Note: the exemption is in art. 42.3.f, not art. 7.
- **Deferral** of the taxable part above the exemption: imputed in the year of a listing or of disposal of the shares, and at the latest when **10 years** have passed from delivery (LIRPF art. 14.2.m).
- **Valuation**: the price paid by an independent third party in the last capital increase of the previous year; otherwise market value at delivery (LIRPF art. 43.1.1º.g).
- The company withholds and reports as payroll in kind; see `spanish-payroll`. Accounting for share-based payments: PGC NRV 17; see `spanish-industry-saas`.

## 6. Founders and staff: social security and impatriados

- **Pluriactividad bonus:** a founder in RETA with effective control of an emergente who is also employed elsewhere gets a **100 % bonus** of the contribution on the minimum base for up to **3 years**, while the pluriactividad lasts (Ley 20/2007 art. 38 quinquies, from Ley 28/2022 DF 4ª). Not compatible with the bonuses of arts. 31-32 of the same law.
- **Impatriados regime** (LIRPF art. 93, as amended): people who become Spanish residents after **5 years** without residence (previously 10) may be taxed for the year of arrival and the next **5** under IRNR rules: 24 % on employment income up to 600,000 euros, 47 % above. It covers, among others, employees, administrators, entrepreneurs and highly qualified professionals working for emergentes or in R&D, and may extend to spouse and children. See `spanish-payroll`.

## 7. Foreign founders and remote workers

- **Entrepreneur residence** (Ley 14/2013 arts. 69-70): based on a favourable ENISA report on the business plan, requested through the UGE (Unidad de Grandes Empresas y Colectivos Estratégicos).
- **International remote work visa** ("nómadas digitales", Ley 14/2013 arts. 74 bis-74 quinquies): non-EU nationals working remotely for companies outside Spain; self-employed may work for Spanish clients up to **20 %** of their activity; need a degree or three years' experience and at least three months' relationship with the foreign employer or clients. Visa up to 1 year; residence permit up to 3 years, renewable for 2. The income threshold (commonly quoted as 200 % of the SMI) is set outside the law (unverified). Immigration questions go to a lawyer.

## 8. Ask the founder

- Founded less than 5 years ago (7 in strategic sectors)? Any dividends paid?
- At least 60 % of staff on Spanish contracts?
- Does the business model rely on technology or innovation that ENISA would see as scalable?
- Planning an option plan for employees? When will options be granted relative to certification?
- Is any founder also employed elsewhere (pluriactividad bonus) or moving to Spain (impatriados)?
