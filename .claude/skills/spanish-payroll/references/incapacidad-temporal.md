# Incapacidad temporal (IT, sick leave)

Who pays which day, how much, and how to book it. Sources: LGSS arts. 169-176; percentages from Decreto 3158/1966 art. 2 and Orden de 13 de octubre de 1967 (confirmed by the Seguridad Social CAISS guide, November 2024); *pago delegado* under Orden de 25 de noviembre de 1966.

## Who pays and how much

**Contingencias comunes** (enfermedad común, accidente no laboral):

| Days of baja | Benefit | Paid by | Cost borne by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-3 | Nothing by law | (convenio may top up) | Employer if convenio says so |
| 4-15 | 60 % of base reguladora | Employer | Employer (LGSS art. 173.1) |
| 16-20 | 60 % | Employer in pago delegado | INSS or mutua (recovered) |
| 21 onward | 75 % | Employer in pago delegado | INSS or mutua (recovered) |

**Contingencias profesionales** (accidente de trabajo, enfermedad profesional): the employer pays the full salary for the day of the baja; from the next day 75 % of the base reguladora, paid by the employer in pago delegado and borne by the mutua or INSS (LGSS art. 173.1).

**Special situations of art. 169.1.a** (menstruación incapacitante secundaria, interrupción del embarazo, gestación from week 39): benefit from the day after the baja, borne by the entity covering contingencias comunes; employer pays the salary of the day of the baja (LGSS art. 173.1, in force since 1 June 2023).

- **Requirements** for contingencias comunes: 180 days of cotización in the last five years (LGSS art. 172); none for accidents or professional illness.
- **Duration**: 365 days, extendable by 180 (LGSS art. 169.1).
- **Convenio top-ups** (*complemento de IT*) to 100 % are common. They are company cost, not recoverable, excluded from the cotización base (LGSS art. 147.2.d) and subject to IRPF.

## Base reguladora

Base de cotización for contingencias comunes of the month before the baja ÷ days of that month (30 for monthly salaries). Overtime is added for IT from contingencias profesionales using the average of the previous year. Example: previous month base 2 566,67 € → 85,56 €/day; days 4-20 = 51,33 €/day; from day 21 = 64,17 €/day.

## Cotización during IT

The employment relationship is suspended but cotización continues: same base as the month before the baja, employer and employee shares as usual (LGSS art. 144.4 and the Orden de cotización art. 6). The employee's share is deducted from the IT benefit. The AT/EP rate of the activity or occupation still applies.

## Pago delegado and recovery

- The employer pays the benefit with the nómina and deducts the amounts borne by INSS/mutua (from day 16, or day 2 for AT/EP) in the TGSS liquidation of the same period (*compensación*), via the IT data the TGSS holds from the parte de baja.
- Partes de baja, confirmación and alta are sent electronically by the health service to INSS; the employer no longer forwards paper copies (RD 625/2014 as amended by RD 1060/2022). Employer reports salary data through the FIE file when the TGSS asks.
- Days 4-15 are the employer's own cost and are not compensated.

## IRPF

IT benefits paid by the employer in pago delegado are employment income subject to withholding at the employee's normal tipo (RIRPF art. 75.1.a; they are reported in modelo 190 together with salary).

## Booking

| Item | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Days 4-15 (employer's cost) and convenio top-up | **640** (some companies use **642** for the legal 60 %; keep one policy) | **465** / **4751** / **476** |
| Benefit from day 16 paid on behalf of INSS/mutua | **471** | **465** (net), **4751** (IRPF), **476** (employee share) |
| Compensation in the TGSS liquidation | **476** | **471** |

Worked month (contingencia común, 31-day month, baja from day 10, base reguladora 85,56 €/day): days 10-12 nothing; days 13-24 (4th-15th) 12 × 51,33 = 615,96 € employer cost; days 25-29 (16th-20th) 5 × 51,33 = 256,65 €; days 30-31 (21st+) 2 × 64,17 = 128,34 €. Debit **471** 384,99 € recovered through the liquidation.

## Never guess

- Whether the baja is común or profesional: the parte states it; the mutua may dispute it.
- Whether the convenio pays from day 1 and how much.
- Relapses (*recaídas*): a new baja for the same cause within 180 days continues the same IT period (LGSS art. 169.2), so no new days 1-3 and the counters continue.
