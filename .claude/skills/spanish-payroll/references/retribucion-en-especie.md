# Retribución en especie (benefits in kind)

IRPF valuation and exemptions, the ingreso a cuenta, and cotización. Sources: LIRPF arts. 42-43; RIRPF arts. 43-48 bis and 102; LGSS art. 147. Labour limit: especie may not exceed 30 % of total salary (ET art. 26.1).

## 1. Concept

Especie is the private use, consumption or obtaining of goods, rights or services free or below market price (LIRPF art. 42.1). If the company gives cash to buy the item, it is cash pay, not especie.

**Not especie** (art. 42.2): training required by the job (RIRPF art. 44; travel for that training follows RIRPF art. 9); accident-at-work and civil liability insurance for the employee.

## 2. Exempt especie (LIRPF art. 42.3)

| Benefit | Exempt limit | Conditions | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Comedor de empresa, direct (own canteen) | Full | Working days; not on days with exempt manutención dietas | art. 42.3.a; RIRPF art. 45.1 |
| Comedor via indirect formulas (cheque/tarjeta restaurante, delivery to workplace or telework location) | **11 €/day**; excess is especie | Working days only; not on dieta days; nominative, numbered, not transferable or refundable, no carry-over; only in hostelería; company keeps a per-employee register | art. 42.3.a; RIRPF art. 45.2 |
| Social and cultural facilities; *primer ciclo de educación infantil* (guardería 0-3) in own premises or contracted with authorised centres | Full | Includes *cheque guardería* paid to the centre | art. 42.3.b |
| Seguro de enfermedad | **500 €/year per person** (employee, spouse, descendants); 1 500 € per person with disability | Health cover only; excess is especie | art. 42.3.c; RIRPF art. 46 |
| Schooling of employees' children at an educational employer | Full | Authorised centres, free or below price | art. 42.3.d |
| Public transport (ticket/tarjeta transporte) | **1 500 €/year**; indirect cards max 136,36 €/month | Home-to-work, public collective transport; cards nominative, numbered, only for transport tickets | art. 42.3.e; RIRPF art. 46 bis |
| Shares of the company or group to active employees | **12 000 €/year**; **50 000 €/year** in an *empresa emergente* (Ley 28/2022) | Same offer to all staff (not required for startups); employee + family ≤ 5 %; hold 3 years | art. 42.3.f; RIRPF art. 43 |

Stock options are not covered by the 12 000 € exemption until shares are delivered; the gain on exercise is employment income in the exercise year, eligible for the 30 % reduction of LIRPF art. 18.2 if not exercisable within two years of grant (limits in art. 18.2; read before applying).

The dietas exemption (RIRPF art. 9) is not especie; see `spanish-travel-expenses`.

## 3. Valuation of taxable especie (LIRPF art. 43.1)

| Benefit | Value |
|---|---|
| Housing owned by the payer | 10 % of valor catastral (5 % if revised within the last 10 years); capped at 10 % of other employment income |
| Housing not owned by the payer | Cost to the payer, including taxes; not less than the owned-housing value |
| Car: delivery | Acquisition cost for the payer, including taxes |
| Car: use | 20 % a year of acquisition cost (or of market value new if not owned), reducible for efficient vehicles (§4) |
| Loans below interés legal del dinero | Difference with the legal rate (3,25 % in 2025 and 2026, unverified) |
| Manutención, hospedaje, viajes; insurance premiums; studies of the employee's family | Cost to the payer, including taxes |
| Pension plan contributions; dependency insurance | Their amount |
| Goods or services of the company's own trade | Not below public offer price minus ordinary discounts (discounts up to 15 % and 1 000 €/year are ordinary) (art. 43.1.1º.f; RIRPF art. 48) |
| Startup shares | Price paid by an independent third party in the last capital increase of the previous year, else market value (art. 43.1.1º.g) |

The ingreso a cuenta is added to the value unless passed on to the employee (art. 43.2).

## 4. Company car

- Use for private purposes is especie. Value = 20 % a year × cost (owned) or × market value new (leased/renting), pro rata to the months available and to the private-use share. Tribunal Supremo and DGT accept a split between business and private use, but the test is **availability** for private use, not actual use; without a usage record, assume full availability outside working hours (DGT doctrine; unverified as a rule).
- Reduction for efficient vehicles (RIRPF art. 48 bis):

| Vehicle | Reduction |
|---|---|
| Euro 6, CO2 ≤ 120 g/km, market value new ≤ 25 000 € | 15 % |
| As above and hybrid or GLP/GNC, value ≤ 35 000 € | 20 % |
| Battery electric (BEV), extended-range (E-REV) | 30 % |
| Plug-in hybrid (PHEV) with ≥ 15 km range, value ≤ 40 000 € | 30 % |

- Fuel, insurance, tolls paid for private trips: especie at cost.
- VAT on the car is a separate question (LIVA art. 95.Tres; `spanish-vat`).

## 5. Ingreso a cuenta

- The company pays an ingreso a cuenta = tipo de retención of the employee × especie value (RIRPF art. 102), with the modelo 111 for the period the especie is satisfied.
- If the employee bears it, deduct it in the nómina. If the company bears it, it is additional especie (gross-up: the modelo 190 shows especie value and ingreso a cuenta separately; the employee includes both).
- Exempt especie has no ingreso a cuenta but is reported in modelo 190 only when indirect formulas are used (RIRPF art. 108.2.c).

## 6. Seguridad Social: especie always cotiza

LGSS art. 147 includes all especie in the base; the IRPF exemptions do **not** carry over. Cheque comida, seguro médico, guardería, transport card and share plans all cotiza at their value (RD 2064/1995 art. 23 values especie by IRPF rules). Only the items in art. 147.2 are excluded (`cotizaciones-2026.md`).

## 7. Flexible remuneration plans

Salary sacrifice (*retribución flexible*) swaps cash for exempt especie. Valid if agreed in writing, prospective, within the 30 % especie cap and without reducing cash pay below the SMI. The IRPF exemption applies; cotización does not change because especie cotiza anyway. Do not apply a swap retroactively.

PGC places voluntary social benefits (canteen, insurance, nursery) in **649** Otros gastos sociales; specific salary-in-kind agreed as part of pay may go to **640**. Choose one per company and keep it.
