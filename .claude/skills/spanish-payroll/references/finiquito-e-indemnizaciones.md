# Finiquito and indemnizaciones

What goes into the final settlement, how severance is computed, and how much is free of IRPF and cotización. Sources: ET arts. 40, 41, 49-56 and DT 11ª; LIRPF arts. 7.e and 18.2; RIRPF art. 73; LGSS art. 147.2.c.

## 1. Finiquito items

| Item | Cotiza | IRPF |
|---|---|---|
| Salary for days worked in the final month | Yes, normal liquidation | Yes |
| Unpaid share of pagas extra accrued | Already cotized through the monthly prorrata; not again | Yes |
| Vacation days accrued and not taken | Yes, complementary liquidation for the vacation days after the end date (LGSS art. 147.1) | Yes |
| Pending pluses, commissions, overtime | Yes | Yes |
| Salary in lieu of notice not given by the employer (e.g. 15 days in despido objetivo, ET art. 53.1.c) | Yes (salary) | Yes |
| Indemnización | Excluded up to the statutory amount | Exempt up to the statutory amount, cap 180 000 € |
| Minus: anticipos, unearned vacation taken, notice not given by the employee if the convenio provides a deduction | | |

The employer must deliver a proposed settlement (*propuesta del documento de liquidación*) with the notice of termination, and the employee may ask for a workers' representative to be present when signing (ET art. 49.2).

## 3. Computing the indemnización

- **Daily salary**: annual gross salary at the time of dismissal (including prorrata of pagas extra and regular pluses, salary in kind) ÷ 365. Courts use the salary the employee was entitled to, not the one actually paid if lower.
- **Seniority**: from start date to end date, periods under a year prorated by months (ET art. 53.1.b, 56.1); chained contracts in fraud of law may count from the first.
- Example: 2 566,67 € monthly incl. prorrata → 30 800 €/year → 84,38 €/day. Six years in despido objetivo: 20 × 6 × 84,38 = 10 125,60 €. Improcedente: 33 × 6 × 84,38 = 16 707,24 €.

## 4. IRPF exemption (LIRPF art. 7.e)

- Exempt: the indemnización **in the amount compulsory under the ET** or court execution rules, not an amount set by convenio, agreement or contract. Overall cap **180 000 €**.
- **Despido improcedente**: exempt up to 33 (or 45) days only if the improcedencia is recognised in the *acto de conciliación* before the SMAC (administrative conciliation service) or by a court. Recognition in the dismissal letter alone does not qualify since 2014 (art. 7.e, third paragraph). Ask for the acta.
- **Despido colectivo** and **objetivo for economic, technical, organisational or production reasons (ET art. 52.c)**: exempt up to the improcedente limits (33 days, 24 months) even without conciliation.
- **End of temporary contract**: the 12 days are compulsory under ET art. 49.1.c and therefore exempt.
- **Real disconnection**: the exemption fails if within three years the employee works again for the same company or a related one (LIS art. 18 / Código de Comercio art. 42 group), unless shown to be genuine (RIRPF art. 73).
- **Mutual agreement**, voluntary early-retirement plans and amounts above the statutory scale: taxable.
- **Taxable excess**: 30 % reduction if generated over more than two years and not periodic (LIRPF art. 18.2), on a maximum of 300 000 € a year; the cap falls for severance between 700 000 € and 1 000 000 € and is zero above (art. 18.2, third paragraph).
- **Withholding**: none on the exempt part; the excess (after the 30 % reduction) at the employee's tipo, recomputed for the final payment.
- Modelo 190: exempt indemnización reported under clave L (subclave per the modelo 190 instructions), taxable part under clave A.

## 5. Cotización

Indemnizaciones por despido are excluded from the base up to the statutory amount; the excess cotiza. When the contract ends before the conciliation, the amount up to the improcedente scale is excluded unless it is a voluntary-exit plan (LGSS art. 147.2.c).

## 7. Booking and worked example

Example: despido objetivo (economic causes), end date 15 May 2026, 6 years, monthly salary 2 200 € (14 pagas), 5 vacation days pending, tipo 13,50 %.

| Item | € |
|---|---|
| Salary 1-15 May | 1 100,00 |
| Pending paga extra prorrata (June paga, accrual Jan-June: 4,5 months) | 1 650,00 |
| 5 vacation days (2 200 × 14 / 365 × 5) | 421,92 |
| Indemnización 20 days × 6 years, daily 84,38 € | 10 125,60 |
| Employee cotización on salary and vacation (illustrative 6,50 %) | −98,92 |
| IRPF 13,50 % on 3 171,92 | −428,21 |
| Net to pay | 12 770,39 |
