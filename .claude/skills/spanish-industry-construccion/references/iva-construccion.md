## 1. Service or supply of goods

An *ejecución de obra* (works contract) is normally a supply of services (LIVA art. 11.Dos.6.º). It becomes a **supply of goods** when it has as its object the construction or rehabilitation of a building and the builder supplies materials whose cost exceeds **40 % of the taxable base** (art. 8.Dos.1.º).

Why it matters:
- The reverse charge in art. 84.Uno.2.º f applies to works that are services. Under DGT doctrine, when the works qualify as a supply of goods under art. 8.Dos.1.º, the reverse charge in letter f) does not apply and the builder charges IVA (unverified: consulta reference not checked; ask an adviser where materials are near 40 %).
- Rate rules for works that produce goods at a reduced rate: art. 91.Tres extends the goods rates in art. 91.Uno.1 and 91.Dos.1 to works that are services with that immediate result, except construction or rehabilitation of VPO, which stays at 10 % under art. 91.Uno.3.

"Rehabilitación" has a strict definition (art. 20.Uno.22.º B): more than 50 % of the project cost on structural work, façades, roofs or analogous/connected works, and total cost above 25 % of the building's purchase price (if bought in the two previous years) or market value, excluding land. Ordinary refurbishment that fails this test is not rehabilitation.

## 2. Inversión del sujeto pasivo in construction

**Rule (LIVA art. 84.Uno.2.º f).** The recipient is the taxpayer for *ejecuciones de obra*, with or without materials, and for the *cesión de personal* to carry them out, under contracts directly between the **promotor** and the **contratista** whose object is the **urbanización de terrenos** or the **construcción o rehabilitación de edificaciones**. It also applies when the recipient is the main contractor or another subcontractor in the same chain.

| Case | Reverse charge? |
|---|---|
| Builder constructs a new building for a developer (promotor) | Yes |
| Subcontractor (electrician, plumber, structure) works for the main contractor on that building | Yes |
| Sub-subcontractor works for a subcontractor | Yes |
| Contractor rehabilitates a building (as defined in art. 20.Uno.22.º B) for its owner who acts as promotor and as a business | Yes |
| Reform of a shop, office or home that is not *rehabilitación* | No: IVA charged normally |
| Work for a private individual building their own home (not acting as a business) | No: the recipient must act as empresario o profesional (art. 84.Uno.2.º opening paragraph) |
| Supply of materials only, or hire of machinery without operator | No |
| Architects, engineers, project management | No: not an ejecución de obra |
| Hire of staff to a contractor for the works (cesión de personal) | Yes |
| Works for a public administration as promotor | Yes if the administration acts as a business for that operation; otherwise no (ask) |

**Communications (RIVA art. 24 quater).** The promotor must communicate expressly and verifiably to the main contractor that it acts as a business and that the works are part of an urbanisation, construction or rehabilitation process (art. 24 quater.3). Each contractor must communicate the second circumstance to its subcontractors (art. 24 quater.4). Keep the communication on file with the contract. Without it, the subcontractor carries the risk of a wrong invoice.

**Who bears an error.** If the supplier wrongly charges IVA, the recipient cannot deduct it and must request a corrected invoice; if the supplier wrongly omits IVA, AEAT may assess the supplier. Ask for the communication before issuing the first invoice.

**Renuncia and other building deliveries.** The reverse charge also applies to deliveries of property in insolvency proceedings, to exempt deliveries where the seller waives the exemption, and to deliveries in execution of a guarantee (art. 84.Uno.2.º e). See §5.

## 3. Rates on construction work

| Work | Rate | Conditions | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Construction or rehabilitation of buildings mainly for housing, contract between promotor and contratista | 10 % | 50 %+ of the built area is housing; includes premises, annexes, garages and services in it | art. 91.Uno.3.1.º |
| Supply with installation of kitchen and bathroom units and built-in wardrobes for those buildings, contract with the promotor | 10 % | — | art. 91.Uno.3.2.º |
| Garages for a comunidad de propietarios on common land, max 2 spaces per owner | 10 % | — | art. 91.Uno.3.3.º |
| Subcontracted work under a 10 % housing contract | 21 % by reverse charge | The 10 % requires a direct contract with the promotor | art. 91.Uno.3.1.º, 84.Uno.2.º f |
| Construction of VPO | 10 % (not 4 %) | art. 91.Tres second paragraph | art. 91.Uno.3 |
| Construction of commercial, industrial or office buildings | 21 % (reverse charge if promotor-contratista) | — | art. 90 |
| Renovation of a home meeting art. 91.Uno.2.10.º | 10 % | §4 | art. 91.Uno.2.10.º |
| Any other repair, maintenance or reform | 21 % | — | art. 90 |

## 4. Renovation and repair of homes at 10 %

LIVA art. 91.Uno.2.10.º, wording in force from **1 December 2026** (RDL 29/2026 art. 7.Tres; convalidated 12-11-2026). All conditions must hold:

| Condition | Before 1-12-2026 | From 1-12-2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Recipient | Individual not acting as a business who uses the home privately, or a comunidad de propietarios | Same, **plus** homes let as the tenant's habitual residence, whoever the landlord is |
| Payment | No condition | Price paid by credit or debit card, bank transfer, *cheque nominativo* or deposit into the builder's bank account |
| Age of the home | Construction or rehabilitation finished at least two years before the works start | Same |
| Materials | Builder supplies no materials, or their cost does not exceed 40 % of the taxable base | Same |

Practical points:
- "Materials" means the goods incorporated into the work; labour, machinery and transport are not materials. Keep the cost breakdown to prove the 40 % test.
- The placement of the payment condition, in a paragraph after the extension to comunidades and rented homes, suggests it applies to all recipients; confirm with an adviser (the text does not say "only for rented homes").
- A cash-paid renovation invoiced from 1-12-2026 does not qualify and is 21 %.
- The rate follows the devengo date: an advance payment received before 1-12-2026 accrued IVA under the old wording (art. 75.Dos).
- Goods delivered without installation (appliances, furniture) follow their own rate, normally 21 %.

## 5. Land and buildings: first and second deliveries

| Sale | IVA | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Rustic or non-building land | Exempt (art. 20.Uno.20.º); ITP applies | Exemption may be waived (art. 20.Dos) |
| Urbanised land, land being urbanised, building plots (solares) | 21 % + AJD | — |
| First delivery of a new building by its promotor | 10 % homes (with up to 2 garage spaces and annexes, art. 91.Uno.1.7.º); 21 % commercial premises | Not a first delivery if the promotor used or let it for 2+ years, unless the buyer was the user (art. 20.Uno.22.º A) |
| First delivery of VPO régimen especial / promoción pública by the promotor; from 1-12-2026 also protected homes with permanent or indefinite qualification | 4 % | art. 91.Dos.1.6.º |
| Second and subsequent deliveries | Exempt; ITP applies | art. 20.Uno.22.º A |
| Second delivery for demolition and new development, or for rehabilitation by the buyer | Not exempt: IVA | art. 20.Uno.22.º A b), c) |
| Second delivery with renuncia a la exención | IVA at 21 % or 10 %, by reverse charge; AJD instead of ITP | art. 20.Dos, 84.Uno.2.º e |

**Renuncia a la exención (art. 20.Dos; RIVA art. 8.1 and 24 quater.1).** The seller may waive the exemption when the buyer is a business entitled to deduct input IVA, fully or partly, on the purchase (or will be, given the intended use). The waiver must be communicated verifiably to the buyer before or at the delivery, for each operation, and backed by a declaration signed by the buyer stating its status and right to deduct. The buyer then self-assesses IVA by reverse charge. Waiving usually replaces a non-deductible ITP (6–11 % by region) with a deductible IVA plus AJD, so it matters for developers and investors with full deduction.

## 6. Devengo: certificaciones and advance payments

- Works that are services accrue IVA when the work is performed (art. 75.Uno.2.º); works that are supplies of goods accrue when the building is put at the buyer's disposal (art. 75.Uno.1.º).
- Any payment before that point accrues IVA on the amount received (art. 75.Dos). In practice each *certificación de obra* paid during the works is an advance payment and accrues IVA when paid; issue the invoice then.
- A certificación issued but not yet paid does not by itself accrue IVA, but most contractors invoice it immediately; the invoice date then drives the 303 period.
- Reverse-charge works that run continuously for more than a year without advance payments accrue on 31 December each year for the part performed (art. 75.Uno.2.º second paragraph).
- Retenciones de garantía withheld by the client are part of the price: the IVA on them accrues with the invoice of the certificación, not when the retention is released (unverified: confirm with DGT doctrine for retentions released after reception).

## 7. Invoices and modelo 303

- An invoice under reverse charge carries no IVA and states "inversión del sujeto pasivo" (RD 1619/2012 art. 6.1.m). See `spanish-invoice-compliance`.
- The recipient self-assesses in modelo 303 as IVA devengado for *otras operaciones con inversión del sujeto pasivo* and deducts the same amount as IVA soportado if entitled (box numbers: see `spanish-vat`).
- Contractors whose output is mostly reverse charge accumulate input IVA and are often in a refund position; consider the monthly refund regime REDEME (RIVA art. 30) with `spanish-vat`.
- Public-works subcontractors must submit invoices to the main contractor through FACeB2B when required (Ley 9/2017 DA 32.ª); RD 238/2026 DT 1.ª gives them 24 months from the Orden HAC/1028/2026 to move to the new e-invoicing system.
