# Hostelería: IVA and sales

Reference for the sales side of a Spanish restaurant, bar, café, catering business or hotel. Rules checked against the consolidated LIVA (Ley 37/1992) on boe.es on 24 November 2026. Scope is *territorio común*; Canarias applies IGIC and Ceuta/Melilla IPSI (see `spanish-vat`).

## 1. The basic split: service or goods

LIVA art. 91.Uno.2.2.º puts at 10 % "los servicios de hostelería, acampamento y balneario, los de restaurantes y, en general, el suministro de comidas y bebidas para consumir en el acto, incluso si se confeccionan previo encargo del destinatario". The provision does not distinguish food from drink, so **everything served to consume on the spot is 10 %, alcohol included**.

When the customer takes the product away, there is no restaurant service; there is an *entrega de bienes* (supply of goods) and each product takes its own rate under art. 91.Uno.1 and 91.Dos.1. The test is whether the customer receives the support services that make it a restaurant supply (table, service, crockery, cleaning), following the CJEU line in C-497/09 *Bog* and others, which the DGT applies. A counter with stools where food is eaten on the spot is restaurant service.

| What the guest gets | Nature | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Meal or drink at a table, bar or terrace | Restaurant service | 10 % |
| Prepared dish to take away | Entrega de bienes, food | 10 % (art. 91.Uno.1.1.º) |
| Bread, milk, cheese, eggs, fruit and vegetables, olive oil sold as such (bakery counter, shop) | Entrega de bienes | 4 % (art. 91.Dos.1.1.º) |
| A sandwich or prepared dish containing 4 % items | Entrega of a prepared food | 10 % (the product, not its ingredients) |

## 2. Drinks

| Drink | Served on the spot | Taken away |
|---|---|---|
| Beer, wine, spirits, cocktails | 10 % | 21 %: alcoholic drinks are excluded from the food rate (art. 91.Uno.1.1.º a) |
| Soft drinks, juices and gaseosas with added sugar or sweeteners | 10 % | 21 % (art. 91.Uno.1.1.º b) |
| Juices and drinks without added sugar or sweeteners | 10 % | 10 % |
| Water (bottled or tap) | 10 % | 10 % (art. 91.Uno.1.4.º) |
| Coffee, tea | 10 % | 10 % |

A bar selling bottles of wine to take away, or a café with a take-away fridge, needs separate TPV articles for the take-away versions. Ask how the TPV is configured before trusting the rate split in the Z-report.

## 3. Take-away, delivery and catering

- **Take-away (para llevar)**: entrega de bienes; food at 10 %, alcohol and sugary drinks at 21 %. Packaging is part of the supply. A separate charge for single-use plastic containers (required by Ley 7/2022 art. 55 for some containers) follows the main supply (unverified: confirm the DGT position on the container charge).
- **Own delivery**: the delivery fee is accessory to the food supply and follows its rate (accessory follows principal, CJEU C-349/96 *CPP*). A delivery fee charged for a basket containing 10 % and 21 % items is split pro rata.
- **Catering**: food delivered and served with staff, tables, crockery at the event is a restaurant service at 10 %. Food simply delivered in trays without service is an entrega de bienes.
- **Ley 1/2025 art. 8** (desperdicio alimentario): restaurants must let customers take away leftovers at no extra cost other than, where applicable, the plastic container charge, and must say so visibly, except at buffets.

## 4. Hotels, hostels and tourist apartments

| Supply | Rate | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Room, with or without breakfast or half board in one price | 10 % | art. 91.Uno.2.2.º |
| Minibar, room service, restaurant and bar | 10 % | consumption on the spot |
| Parking charged separately | 21 % | general rate (unverified: a parking space included in the room price follows the room) |
| Laundry, spa, gym, excursions charged separately | 21 % unless accessory to accommodation and included in the price (unverified for spa: some balneario services fall under art. 91.Uno.2.2.º) | art. 90 |
| Meeting-room hire without catering | 21 % | arrendamiento de local |
| Cancellation fees and no-show charges kept by the hotel | Since CJEU C-295/17 *MEO* and DGT doctrine, treated as consideration for the service, at the room rate (unverified: check the current DGT criterion) | — |

**Tourist apartments and short lets (change from 1 December 2026).** RDL 29/2026 (BOE 7-10-2026, convalidated by the Congreso, Resolución de 12-11-2026) rewrote LIVA art. 20.Uno.23.º e'). From 1-12-2026 the letting of furnished apartments or homes is **not exempt** when either (a) the landlord provides any hotel-type service (restaurant, cleaning, laundry or similar) or (b) the stay for the same tenant does not exceed 30 nights, unless the landlord lives in the home. Such lettings are taxed at 10 % (art. 91.Uno.2.2.º, second paragraph). Before 1-12-2026 only case (a) was taxable. An earlier attempt, RDL 26/2026, was derogated by the Congreso (Resolución de 2-10-2026) and never took effect. A business that becomes taxable gains the right to deduct input IVA and must register for IVA and start filing modelo 303.

## 5. Events, packages and mixed prices

- **Weddings and banquets**: the banquet is a restaurant service at 10 %. Music, flowers, photography or room decoration supplied by the venue for a single price may be a single complex supply at 10 % if they are accessory, or separate supplies at 21 % if the guest could buy them separately; the DGT looks at whether the elements are independent (CJEU C-111/05 *Aktiebolaget NN*, C-41/04 *Levob*). Ask how the contract prices them.
- **Hotel package with excursions or spa**: split the price by market value when the elements are independent.
- **Menu with a drink choice**: a menú del día served on the premises is all 10 %, so no split is needed.
- **Take-away menu combining food and a soft drink with sugar**: split between 10 % and 21 % by relative value.

## 6. Delivery and booking platforms

| Platform question | Usual answer | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Who sells the food to the customer? | Normally the restaurant; the platform acts as an intermediary in the restaurant's name. The restaurant's sale is the full menu price at 10 % (21 % for alcohol and sugary drinks) | Platform terms and the settlement report |
| What is the commission? | A taxable service invoiced by the platform to the restaurant, 21 % | The commission invoice |
| Who invoices the commission? | A Spanish entity charges 21 % IVA; an entity established in another EU country invoices without IVA and the restaurant self-assesses under *inversión del sujeto pasivo* (reverse charge, LIVA art. 84.Uno.2.º and 69.Uno.1.º), and reports it in modelo 349 | The issuer and NIF/VAT number on each invoice; booking platforms are commonly established in other EU states (unverified per platform) |
| What arrives in the bank? | Net payout: sales minus commission, delivery fees, promotions and refunds | Never book the net payout as revenue |

Booking: **4300**/**440** (platform as debtor) for the gross sale, **705** and **477**; commission to **623** or **629** with **472**; payout to **572**. For a non-established platform: **472** and **477** for the same 21 % on the commission.

If the platform buys the food and resells it in its own name (some "dark store" or own-brand models), the restaurant sells to the platform, B2B, and must issue a full invoice. Ask for the contract.

## 7. Propinas and service charges

- **Voluntary tips** given by the customer are not consideration for the service and stay out of the IVA base (DGT consulta general 2174-03 of 11-12-2003; repeated in later consultas such as V2604-10, unverified wording). Do not add them to **705**.
- **Compulsory service charge** ("servicio 10 %" on every bill, not optional): consideration for the service, part of the IVA base at 10 % (LIVA art. 78.Uno). A mandatory charge must also be shown on menus under consumer rules.
- Tips received by card enter the business bank account; book them to a liability (**465** if paid through payroll, or a staff-tips sub-account of **555**) and clear it when paid to staff. Payroll treatment in `personal-y-operaciones.md` §3.

## 8. Tasa turística

Regional and municipal taxes on overnight stays. The guest is the taxpayer and the establishment collects as *sustituto del contribuyente* and pays it over. It is **not revenue**: book it to a sub-account of **475** and show it separately on the invoice. Treatment in the IVA base: the established reading is that a tax whose taxpayer is the guest, collected by the establishment as sustituto, is not part of the IVA base (unverified: no DGT consulta checked for this file; confirm before relying on it).

| Region | Tax | Status on 24-11-2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Catalonia | *Impuesto sobre las estancias en establecimientos turísticos* (Ley 5/2017, amended by Ley 2/2026 of 6-3-2026, BOE 23-3-2026, in force 1-4-2026) | In force. Barcelona city from 1-4-2026: 5-star/luxury 7.00 €, 4-star 3.40 €, tourist homes 4.50 €, youth hostels 1.00 €, other 2.00 € per person and night. Rest of Catalonia 1-4-2026 to 31-3-2027: 4.50 / 1.80 / 1.75 / 0.80 / 0.90 €; from 1-4-2027: 6.00 / 2.40 / 2.50 / 1.00 / 1.20 €. Barcelona surcharge up to 8 €; other municipalities may add up to 4 € for stays from 1-10-2026 (Ley 5/2017 art. 34, 34 bis, 34 ter). Booking made and paid in advance keeps the tariff in force at booking (art. 34.3) |
| Baleares | *Impuesto sobre estancias turísticas* (Ley 2/2016) | In force; roughly 1–4 € per night by category in high season with low-season reductions (unverified amounts: check the current BOIB text) |
| Galicia | Municipal tax enabled by regional law (Santiago de Compostela, A Coruña, Vigo reported) | Unverified: check the municipal ordinance |
| Basque Country | Impuesto sobre estancias turísticas approved by the three territories | Reported to apply from 1-1-2027 (unverified) |
| Other regions | No regional tax known | Check before assuming none applies, especially municipal ordinances |

Exemptions (minors, long stays, health stays) and the number of nights taxed differ by region; read the regional law before configuring the PMS.

## 9. Vouchers, meal cards and invitations

- **Gift vouchers** (bonos regalo): a *bono univalente* (single-purpose: place and rate known) is taxed when sold; a *bono polivalente* (multi-purpose) is taxed when redeemed. The consolidated LIVA has no specific voucher article; these are the rules of Directive (EU) 2016/1065 as applied by the DGT (unverified: confirm the current DGT criterion; see `spanish-vat`).
- **Meal cards and vouchers** (Edenred, Pluxee, Cobee and similar): the restaurant sells to the employee at the full price with 10 % IVA. The card company pays the restaurant net of its commission, which is a separate service invoiced to the restaurant (often exempt as a payment service or taxed at 21 %, depending on the issuer; check each invoice).
- **Free meals to guests** (invitaciones, a free shot): part of the sale or a promotion. If given free with no link to a sale they are an *autoconsumo* (LIVA art. 9 and 12) taxable at cost, except free promotional samples and advertising objects (art. 7.2.º and 7.4.º; a free tapa is not a "sample" in that sense). Staff meals are in `personal-y-operaciones.md` §4.

| Account | Use |
|---|---|
| **700** | Shop sales of goods (take-away shop, wine shop) |
