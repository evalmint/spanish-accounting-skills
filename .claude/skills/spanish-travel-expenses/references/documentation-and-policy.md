## 1. Burden of proof

- The **payer** must prove day, place and reason of each trip for which exempt dietas or kilometraje are paid (RIRPF art. 9.A.3.a). The Tribunal Supremo placed the AEAT's request for that proof on the employer (STS 429/2020, STS 1080/2021). Without it, the AEAT re-qualifies the payments as salary for the employee and the company faces unpaid withholding and cotización.
- Useful evidence: approved travel request, calendar, client visit reports, hotel invoices, tickets, fuel and toll receipts with location, delivery notes, minutes.
- For kilometraje: origin, destination, km and purpose per trip; a monthly flat km amount without detail is salary.

## 2. Hoja de gastos contents

| Field | Why |
|---|---|
| Employee, NIF, department | Link to payroll and modelo 190 |
| Dates and times of departure and return | Overnight stay decides 53,34 vs 26,67 € |
| Destination municipality and the employee's workplace and residence municipalities | Municipality test for manutención |
| Purpose (client, project, event) | Business reason; project allocation |
| Nights with hotel invoice | Estancia exempt only if justified |
| Km, route, vehicle | Kilometraje |
| Receipts attached, each with amount, IVA and whether in the company's NIF | IVA deduction |
| Meals provided by others (client, conference) | Policy: reduce allowance |
| Advance received | Settle **544** |
| Approval by the manager, date | Internal control |

## 3. Invoices and digital receipts

- For IVA deduction, the document must be a full invoice or a simplified invoice with the company's NIF and address and the IVA shown (RD 1619/2012 arts. 6-7). Train, air and hotel booking platforms can issue invoices in the company's name: set the company's billing profile.
- Paper receipts may be digitised and the paper destroyed only with AEAT-homologated digitisation software (*digitalización certificada*, Orden EHA/962/2007; unverified for current homologation rules). Ordinary photos of tickets do not replace the original for tax purposes.
- Invoices received from Spanish issuers increasingly come through Verifactu-compliant systems and, in future, mandatory B2B e-invoicing; see `spanish-invoice-compliance` and `spanish-e-invoicing` for dates.

## 4. Retention periods

- Accounting records and supporting documents: six years (Código de Comercio art. 30).
- Tax: four-year limitation period (LGT art. 66), longer while the AEAT may still verify credits or losses carried forward (LGT art. 66 bis).
- Seguridad Social: four years for cotización debts (LGSS art. 24).

## 5. Reporting obligations

| Item | Where |
|---|---|
| Exempt dietas and kilometraje paid to employees | Modelo 190 (exempt income key); CRA file to TGSS |
| Taxable excess | Nómina: IRPF (modelo 111, 190) and cotización |
| Deductible Spanish IVA | Modelo 303, libro registro de facturas recibidas (or SII) |
| Foreign VAT | Modelo 360 by 30 September of the following year |
| Atenciones a clientes | IS return: excess over 1 % of turnover as a permanent difference (modelo 200) |

## 6. Travel policy points

A written policy reduces disputes and supports the proof:

- Who approves trips; booking channels and billing profile with the company's NIF.
- Daily allowance amounts (equal to RIRPF art. 9 avoids taxable excess) or actual-cost reimbursement with caps.
- Reduction of the allowance when meals are provided.
- Km rate (0,26 € avoids taxable excess) and when to use public transport.
- Hotel category caps; no private nights or companions at company cost.
- Company card rules: no private use, receipts within X days, monthly reconciliation.
- Deadline for expense reports and advances.
- Treatment of administradores and socios (no art. 9 exemption for the cargo).

## 7. Frequent errors

| Error | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Monthly flat "dieta" without trips | Salary: IRPF and cotización, surcharges |
| Dietas paid for trips to the employee's own municipality | Manutención taxable |
| Long postings beyond nine months still paid as exempt dietas | Taxable from the start of the tenth month (and AEAT may argue earlier if the destination is the real workplace) |
| Dietas to administradores treated as exempt | Taxable at 35 % / 19 % |
| IVA deducted on client meals | Regularise; LIVA art. 96.Uno.5º |
| IVA deducted on tickets without company NIF | Regularise |
| Hotel above 400 € with only a simplified invoice | IVA not supported |
| Meal vouchers on days with exempt dietas | Voucher becomes taxable (RIRPF art. 45.1.2º) |
| Exempt dietas missing from modelo 190 and CRA | Information penalties; TGSS queries |

## 8. Per-trip checklist

1. Traveller type: employee, administrador, autónomo.
2. Workplace and residence municipalities vs destination.
3. Dates, overnight stays, months in the same place.
4. Meals provided by others.
5. Allowance vs limits; excess to the nómina.
6. Invoices in the company's NIF; IVA rate; atenciones excluded.
7. Foreign VAT: claim or gross.
8. Entry proposed with accounts per `bookkeeping-entries.md`; user approval before posting.
