# IVA on business travel

Rates, deduction rules and documents for travel costs. Sources: LIVA (Ley 37/1992) arts. 22, 91, 95-97, 119, 141-147; RD 1619/2012 (Reglamento de facturación) arts. 4, 6, 7. Territorio común; Canarias, Ceuta and Melilla at the end.

## Rates

| Service in Spain | IVA | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Hotel and other accommodation | 10 % | LIVA art. 91.Uno.2.2º |
| Restaurant, café, meals to consume on the spot | 10 % | LIVA art. 91.Uno.2.2º |
| Passenger transport: taxi, train, bus, domestic flight, ferry | 10 % | LIVA art. 91.Uno.2.1º |
| International air or sea passenger transport (to or from outside the Spanish VAT territory) | Exempt | LIVA art. 22.Trece |
| Hire car, fuel, parking, motorway tolls, conference fees (general) | 21 % | LIVA art. 90 |
| Travel agency under the special regime (REAV) | IVA not shown | LIVA arts. 141-147 |

Alcoholic drinks served in a restaurant follow the restaurant service rate (10 %), as a supply of meals and drinks to consume on the spot.

## When IVA is deductible

All conditions must hold:

1. **Business use and IS/IRPF deductibility**: IVA on *servicios de desplazamiento o viajes, hostelería y restauración* is deductible only if the cost is a deductible expense for IRPF or IS (LIVA art. 96.Uno.6º). Private or family parts of a trip: no deduction.
2. **Not atenciones a clientes, asalariados o terceros**: meals, gifts and entertainment for clients, suppliers or staff are excluded in full (LIVA art. 96.Uno.5º), even though IS allows atenciones up to 1 % of turnover. A staff member's own meal on a business trip is a travel cost, not an atención.
3. **Not food, drink or recreational services as such** (LIVA art. 96.Uno.3º-4º): shows, leisure.
4. **Valid invoice in the company's name** (LIVA art. 97.Uno): a full invoice, or a *factura simplificada* that also carries the recipient's NIF and address and the IVA amount shown separately (RD 1619/2012 art. 7.2). A till receipt without the company's NIF does not support deduction.
5. Within the four-year period (LIVA art. 99-100) and subject to prorrata if the company has exempt activity.

## Factura simplificada limits (RD 1619/2012 art. 4)

| Case | Maximum, IVA included |
|---|---|
| General | 400 € |
| Restaurants, bars, cafés; passenger transport and luggage; parking; motorway tolls (among others in art. 4.2) | 3 000 € |
| Hotels | Not in the 3 000 € list: above 400 € the hotel must issue a full invoice (unverified whether a hotel restaurant bill falls under the restaurant item) |

## Vehicles

- Hire car (passenger car): IVA presumed 50 % deductible unless the company proves a higher business use (LIVA art. 95.Tres.2ª); 100 % for specific uses listed there (sales representatives, driving schools, transport of passengers for consideration, etc.).
- Fuel, repairs and parking for that car follow the same percentage (LIVA art. 95.Tres.4ª).
- An employee's own car paid at 0,26 €/km: no IVA to deduct (no invoice to the company).

## Travel agencies

- Under the REAV the agency's invoice does not show IVA and the client cannot deduct any (LIVA art. 141 ff.).
- For business clients the agency may apply the general regime operation by operation; then the invoice shows IVA on each component and normal deduction rules apply (LIVA art. 147).
- Ask the agency for general-regime invoicing when the company wants to deduct hotel IVA.

## Foreign VAT

- Foreign VAT is never deducted in the Spanish modelo 303.
- **EU countries**: refund through the AEAT portal with **modelo 360**, for each Member State, by **30 September** of the year after the expense (LIVA art. 119; RIVA art. 31; Directive 2008/9/EC). Each country applies its own deduction limits (many exclude hotels or meals). Book the claim on **4709** only when it is likely to be paid; otherwise the gross cost goes to **629**.
- **Non-EU countries**: refund only where there is reciprocity and with a representative (LIVA art. 119 bis); in practice book gross.

## Canarias, Ceuta and Melilla

- Canarias: hotels and restaurants charge IGIC (general rate 7 %; check the Ley 20/1991 rate schedule), not IVA. IGIC cannot be deducted in the Spanish 303; a company established in the peninsula without IGIC registration books it as cost (unverified for refund procedures under IGIC).
- Ceuta and Melilla: IPSI; same treatment as cost.
- Air transport between the peninsula and Canarias: the part outside the VAT territory is exempt (LIVA art. 22.Trece covers routes to or from outside the territory; check the ticket breakdown).

## Common cases

| Expense | IVA treatment |
|---|---|
| Hotel in Spain, full invoice to the company | Deduct 10 % |
| Hotel breakfast billed to the room | Part of the accommodation service if included; deduct with the hotel invoice |
| Employee's dinner alone on the trip, factura simplificada with company NIF | Deduct 10 % |
| Same, ticket without NIF | No deduction; gross cost |
| Lunch with a client | No deduction (art. 96.Uno.5º); gross to **627** |
| Taxi with receipt naming the company | Deduct 10 % (simplified invoice with NIF) |
| Train or domestic flight booked online in the company's name | Deduct 10 % |
| Flight Madrid-London | Exempt; nothing to deduct |
| Hire car for a three-day sales trip | Deduct 50 % unless 100 % business use is proved |
| Conference ticket in Spain | 21 %, deductible if business |
| Hotel in Germany | German VAT; modelo 360 claim possible, subject to German limits |
