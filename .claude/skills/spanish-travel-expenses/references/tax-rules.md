# Tax rules for business travel

Income-tax and Seguridad Social side of travel: what may be paid free of IRPF and cotización, what becomes salary, and what the company and the autónomo may deduct. IVA is in `vat-on-travel.md`; entries in `bookkeeping-entries.md`.

## 1. Legal basis

- LIRPF art. 17.1.d: dietas and travel allowances are employment income, *except* normal locomoción, manutención and estancia costs within the regulatory limits.
- RIRPF art. 9 sets the limits and conditions (last amended by Orden HFP/792/2023 for the km rate; the consolidated text of 18-11-2026 is unchanged).
- Burden of proof: the **payer** must prove that the amounts correspond to trips on a given day and place for work reasons (RIRPF art. 9.A.3.a, last paragraph). The Tribunal Supremo held that the AEAT must ask the employer, not the employee, for that proof (STS 429/2020 of 18-05-2020, STS 1080/2021 of 22-07-2021; cited in the AEAT Renta 2025 manual).

## 2. Locomoción (RIRPF art. 9.A.2)

Exempt: amounts to compensate travel of an employee who goes outside the factory, office or work centre to work elsewhere, **in the same municipality or another** (AEAT Renta 2025 manual).

| Means | Exempt amount |
|---|---|
| Public transport | Expense justified by invoice or equivalent document |
| Other (own car, motorbike) | 0,26 €/km travelled, if the trip is proved, plus justified tolls and parking |

- 0,26 €/km since 17-07-2023 (Orden HFP/792/2023); 0,19 € before.
- Home to usual workplace is commuting: fully taxable, even between municipalities.
- Fuel paid on top of the km rate is taxable.
- Company transport ticket for commuting is a separate exempt especie up to 1 500 €/year (LIRPF art. 42.3.e; `spanish-payroll`).

## 3. Manutención and estancia (RIRPF art. 9.A.3)

**Conditions**

1. Expense incurred in a **municipality different** from the employee's usual workplace and from their residence.
2. Not a continuous posting of **more than nine months** in the same municipality; vacations, sickness and similar breaks do not interrupt the count (art. 9.A.3, second paragraph).
3. Employer proof of day, place and reason.

**Amounts**

| Situation | Spain | Abroad |
|---|---|---|
| Overnight stay in another municipality: estancia | Justified amount (hotel invoice) | Justified amount |
| Overnight stay: manutención | 53,34 €/day | 91,35 €/day |
| No overnight stay: manutención | 26,67 €/day | 48,08 €/day |

- Manutención is a flat allowance: no receipts needed for the exemption, but proof of the trip is.
- A day with an overnight stay counts at 53,34 €; the return day without a further overnight stay counts at 26,67 € (common practice; the RIRPF ties the amount to whether the person overnighted, unverified for return days).
- Estancia must be justified by the hotel invoice; without it, only manutención is exempt.
- Meals in the employee's own municipality or at the usual workplace: never exempt manutención (but a company canteen or meal voucher may be exempt especie, not on dieta days, RIRPF art. 45).

**Example.** Employee based in Madrid, trip to Barcelona Monday 07:00 to Wednesday 20:00, two hotel nights paid by the employee (300 € with invoice), company pays 60 €/day manutención.

| Item | Paid | Exempt | Taxable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hotel | 300,00 | 300,00 | 0 |
| Manutención Mon, Tue (overnight) | 120,00 | 106,68 | 13,32 |
| Manutención Wed (no overnight) | 60,00 | 26,67 | 33,33 |
| **Total** | 480,00 | 433,35 | **46,65** |

The 46,65 € goes into the nómina as salary with IRPF and cotización.

## 4. Special cases

- **Mobile or itinerant work centres**: staff hired specifically for them get the same regime for trips to a municipality other than their habitual residence (art. 9.A.4).
- **Road haulage drivers**: estancia up to 15 €/day in Spain and 25 €/day abroad without justifying the amount (art. 9.A.3.a.1º).
- **Flight crew**: manutención up to 36,06 €/day in Spain and 66,11 €/day abroad; if both in a day, by the larger number of flights (art. 9.A.3.a.2º).
- **Employees posted abroad**: the excess over the pay they would receive in Spain is an exempt dieta (art. 9.A.3.b.4º), incompatible with the 7.p exemption for work abroad (RIRPF art. 6).
- **Transfer to another municipality requiring a change of residence**: locomoción and manutención of the employee and family during the move, and moving furniture, are exempt (art. 9.B.2).
- **Special labour relationships** (e.g. sales representatives in relación laboral especial) not reimbursed by the company may deduct locomoción and manutención at the same amounts in their own return (art. 9.B.1).
- **Training required by the job**: travel to it follows art. 9 (RIRPF art. 44).

## 5. Who can use art. 9

| Traveller | Art. 9 exemption | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Employee (labour or statutory relationship) | Yes | |
| Administrador or consejero, for the cargo | No | TEAC criterion: art. 9 requires dependence and alienness; reported as TEAC RG 1475/2024 (secondary sources; read the resolution). Some regional courts disagreed. |
| Socio-administrador who also works for the company under a labour or commercial relationship | Only for trips made in the employee/technical function, if that relationship qualifies | DGT V1796-25 (13-10-2025, reported) distinguishes the functions; control of the company weighs against dependence |
| Autónomo | No (own regime, §9) | |

When art. 9 is not available, travel costs the company pays **with invoices in its own name** for a trip made on its behalf may still be a company expense and not income of the administrador (resarcimiento of costs incurred for the company, as reported from DGT doctrine; unverified as a general rule). Flat allowances to administradores are taxable at the administrador rate (35 % / 19 %).

## 6. Direct payment by the company

- Hotel, tickets and hire car paid directly by the company (corporate card, travel agency) for a business trip are company costs, not employee income.
- Meals paid directly with a company card on a business trip: the AEAT treats them like an asignación subject to the same daily limits (unverified; conservative approach is to count them against the manutención limit and to treat any daily allowance on top as taxable).
- Company canteen or meal voucher days must not overlap with exempt dieta days (RIRPF art. 45.1.2º).

## 7. Seguridad Social

LGSS art. 147.2.a-b excludes from the cotización base: public transport with invoice; and locomoción, manutención and estancia in another municipality **in the amount and scope of the IRPF rules**. The excess cotiza in the month paid. A *plus de transporte* or *plus de distancia* for commuting always cotiza.

## 8. Corporate tax (IS) deductibility

- Staff travel costs correlated with the business are deductible under the accounting result (LIS art. 10.3), with an invoice or receipt and proof of the business purpose.
- Atenciones a clientes o proveedores: deductible up to **1 % of net turnover** of the period (LIS art. 15.e); the excess is a permanent difference.
- Staff events and gifts according to usos y costumbres are deductible (LIS art. 15.e).
- Personal or family parts of a trip are a *liberalidad*: not deductible and, for the employee, especie.

## 9. The autónomo's own travel (estimación directa)

- **Own manutención** (LIRPF art. 30.2.5ª.c, from 1-1-2018): deductible if incurred in the course of the activity, in restaurants or hotels, **paid by any electronic means** (card, transfer, Bizum; not cash), and within the RIRPF art. 9 amounts: 26,67 €/day Spain and 48,08 €/day abroad without overnight stay; 53,34 € and 91,35 € with overnight stay. The AEAT manual requires a municipality different from the usual place of work and the residence. Keep the invoice (simplified with NIF is enough).
- **Estancia** (hotel): deductible in full when linked to the activity and justified with an invoice (general rule, LIRPF art. 28 and LIS art. 15).
- **Locomoción**: tickets and taxis deductible with invoice. The 0,26 €/km rate does **not** apply to the autónomo's own car; car costs are deductible only for a vehicle used exclusively for the activity (RIRPF art. 22.2-4; strict for passenger cars).
- **Meals with clients**: deductible as atenciones a clientes if correlated with income, within the 1 % of turnover limit of LIS art. 15.e, which applies through LIRPF art. 28.1 (the AEAT manual's treatment was not re-checked here; unverified). IVA on them is never deductible.
- Estimación objetiva (módulos): no separate deduction.

## 10. Foral territories and Canarias

- País Vasco and Navarra regulate IRPF themselves; their dietas amounts are similar but set in their own regulations (e.g. Normas Forales and Decretos Forales of each territory). Check them for employees whose withholding is foral.
- Seguridad Social is common, so LGSS art. 147.2 applies everywhere and refers to "la normativa estatal reguladora del IRPF".
- Canarias: IGIC instead of IVA on local hotels and meals (`vat-on-travel.md`); IRPF is the common one.
