## What it is

Net income is computed from physical indicators (*signos, índices o módulos*: staff, floor area, electricity, tables, vehicles, load capacity) for activities listed in the yearly ministerial order, not from actual income and expenses (LIRPF art. 31; RIRPF arts. 32-39). It goes with the IVA *régimen simplificado* or the REAGP (LIRPF art. 31.1.2ª). Orders: Orden HAC/1425/2025, de 9 de diciembre (2026) and Orden HAC/1161/2026, de 4 de noviembre (2027). Typical users: bars, restaurants, small shops (*comercio al por menor* under recargo de equivalencia is outside IVA simplificado but may use IRPF módulos), taxis, transport by road, builders' trades, hairdressers.

## Exclusion limits

LIRPF art. 31.1.3ª and RIRPF art. 32.2 set base limits; LIRPF DT 32ª raised them for 2016-2024.

| Limit (previous year) | Base law | DT 32ª (2016-2024) | Applied by the AEAT for 2025 and 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gross income, all activities except farming | 150,000 | 250,000 | 250,000 (see below) |
| Of which invoiced to businesses/professionals | 75,000 | 125,000 | 125,000 |
| Farming, livestock, forestry income | 250,000 | 250,000 | 250,000 |
| Purchases of goods and services (excluding fixed assets) | 150,000 | 250,000 | 250,000 |
| Other exclusions | Activity outside Spain; activity in another method (estimación directa) for any activity; spouse/children/relatives' activities computed jointly when identical or similar (RIRPF art. 32.2.e); professional sección 2ª not listed | | |

Also excluded from módulos: taxpayers whose income must be invoiced to businesses withholding 1 % (RIRPF art. 95.6) above the 125,000 sub-limit, and anyone exceeding the order's own limits per activity (e.g. staff, vehicles).

## The 2025-2026 legal tangle

Facts from the BOE consolidated LIRPF (last update 18/11/2026):

1. DT 32ª, as it stands, extends the 250,000/125,000/250,000 limits only to **2016-2024**.
2. RDL 9/2024 (extension to 2025) was repealed by Congress (Resolución 22 January 2025, BOE-A-2025-1136).
3. RDL 16/2025 (extension to 2025 and 2026, with effect 1 January 2025) was repealed (Resolución 27 January 2026, BOE-A-2026-2024).
4. RDL 2/2026 (same extension, plus a renuncia deadline to 16 February 2026) was repealed (Resolución 26 February 2026, BOE 28/02/2026, BOE-A-2026-4667).

AEAT/DGT position (secondary sources only, not verified on the AEAT site): an AEAT note of 1 April 2026 applying a DGT criterion says the 2026 limits are the same as for 2025 (250,000 / 125,000 / 250,000), that taxpayers who were in módulos in 2025 can stay for 2026, and that renuncias and revocaciones filed while RDL 16/2025 and RDL 2/2026 were in force remain valid (unverified). The legal basis for applying the higher limits in 2025 and 2026 is not visible in the consolidated text.

What the agent should do:

- Present the 250,000/125,000 limits as the administrative criterion for 2025-2026, flag that the statute only covers up to 2024, and recommend the user's adviser confirm before relying on them, especially if income is between 150,000 and 250,000.
- For 2027: nothing extends the limits as of 24 November 2026; watch for a new law or RDL in December 2026.

## Renuncia and revocación

| Element | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| When | In **December** of the year before, via modelo 036 (modelo 037 abolished from 3 February 2025, Orden HAC/1526/2024); or by filing the first quarter's modelo 130 in the April deadline (tacit renuncia) | RIRPF art. 33.1 |
| Starting activity | In the census declaration at the start, or by filing modelo 130 in the first quarter | RIRPF art. 33.1 |
| Lock-in | 3 years in estimación directa; after that, renewed tacitly year by year unless revoked in December | RIRPF art. 33.2 |
| Linked effect | Renuncia to IRPF módulos implies renuncia to IVA simplificado for the same 3 years, and vice versa | RIRPF art. 33.2; RIVA art. 33 |
| Exclusion | Exceeding a limit forces estimación directa from the next year, for 3 years | RIRPF art. 35 |
| 2026 extraordinary window | Renuncia/revocación for 2026 extended to 16 February 2026 by RDL 2/2026 (repealed; filings kept valid per AEAT/DGT, unverified) | |

## Computing the rendimiento

Per the yearly order (RIRPF art. 37):

1. *Rendimiento neto previo*: sum of each module × its annual amount (e.g. per employee, per m², per kW).
2. Minus incentives for employment and investment (index for new staff, etc.).
3. *Rendimiento neto minorado*: minus depreciation per the order's table (only in agricultural activities and some others).
4. Corrective indices (*índices correctores*): small business, start-up, seasonal, small towns.
5. General reduction set in the order (5 % in recent years; check the 2026 order, Orden HAC/1425/2025) (unverified for 2026).
6. Minus *gastos extraordinarios* (fire, flood) if reported to the AEAT.
7. Plus other income (subsidies, etc.).
8. Art. 32 reductions do not apply except 32.1 irregular.

## Modelo 131 and IVA simplificado

- Modelo 131 quarterly: 4 % of the rendimiento neto from the modules on 1 January (3 % with one employee, 2 % with none); farming: 2 % of quarterly income (RIRPF art. 110.1.b-c). Minus withholdings of the quarter (1 % withholding on business clients, RIRPF art. 95.6). Deadlines: 1-20 April, July, October, 1-30 January.
- IVA simplificado: quarterly quota per the order's modules (modelo 303 in the simplificado section), annual summary in modelo 390; deduction of IVA on fixed assets separate. Detail: `spanish-vat`.

## When to leave módulos

| Signal | Why |
|---|---|
| Real margins well below the modules | Paying on fictitious income |
| Large investment planned | Estimación directa deducts depreciation and creates losses; módulos does not |
| Income approaching 150,000 | Statutory limit if the 250,000 criterion is not maintained |
| Many business clients | 125,000 sub-limit and 1 % withholding |
| Incorporating soon | Moving to an SL ends módulos anyway; an SL cannot use módulos (and LIS art. 18.3 imposes full documentation on módulos taxpayers dealing with their own ≥ 25 % companies) |
