## Methods and who uses them

| Method | Who | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Estimación directa normal (EDN) | Default; compulsory if INCN of all activities > 600,000 euros in the previous year, or if the taxpayer renounces simplificada | LIRPF art. 30.1; RIRPF art. 28 |
| Estimación directa simplificada (EDS) | INCN ≤ 600,000 euros previous year, unless renounced; any activity in EDN forces all into EDN | LIRPF art. 30.1; RIRPF art. 28 |
| Estimación objetiva (módulos) | Listed activities under the yearly Orden, within the limits; see `modulos.md` | LIRPF art. 31 |

Renuncia to EDS: in December of the year before (or at the start-of-activity census declaration), binding for 3 years (LIRPF art. 30.1; RIRPF art. 29). Starting activity: the 600,000 test uses the INCN of the first year annualised.

## Computing the rendimiento neto

| Line (modelo 100, apartado E1) | Content |
|---|---|
| Ingresos íntegros | All income from the activity (sales, services, subsidies, self-supply at market value, transfers of current assets); excludes IVA charged |
| − Gastos deducibles | Purchases, staff, RETA quota of the owner, rent, supplies, repairs, services, insurance, financial costs, depreciation, taxes (IAE, IBI of business premises), provisions (EDN only, per IS rules), bad debts (6 months rule) |
| = Diferencia | |
| − Gastos de difícil justificación (EDS only) | 5 % × diferencia, max 2,000 euros (RIRPF art. 30.2ª); not if the art. 32.2 dependent-worker reduction is taken |
| = Rendimiento neto | |
| − Reducciones (art. 32) | 30 % irregular; art. 32.2 low-income or dependent worker; 20 % start-up |
| = Rendimiento neto reducido | Goes into the general base |

Rules: costs need an invoice in the autónomo's name with NIF (RD 1619/2012) and accrual; IS rules apply by reference (LIRPF art. 28.1), so the non-deductible list of LIS art. 15 applies (fines, IRPF itself, gifts beyond the 1 % limit, etc.). Gains and losses on the sale of business assets are not business income but *ganancias patrimoniales* (LIRPF art. 28.2).

## Normal vs simplificada: differences

| Item | EDN | EDS |
|---|---|---|
| Accounting | Commercial activity: Código de Comercio accounts (PGC); professional or non-commercial: libros registro | Libros registro only (RIRPF art. 68) |
| Depreciation | IS tables and methods (LIS art. 12) | Simplified table (Orden de 27 de marzo de 1998, Anexo), straight line; ERD rules (double rate, free depreciation) apply via RIRPF art. 30.1ª |
| Provisions | Deductible under IS rules | Replaced by the 5 %/2,000 flat allowance |
| Difficult-to-justify expenses | None | 5 %, max 2,000 euros |
| Limit | None | INCN ≤ 600,000 |

Gastos de difícil justificación history: 5 %/2,000 since 2015 (RIRPF art. 30.2ª as amended by RD 633/2015); 7 % only for 2023 (LIRPF DA 56ª, Ley 31/2022). For 2024, 2025 and 2026 the rate is 5 % (no DA for those years in the consolidated LIRPF as of 18/11/2026). Ceuta, 2026 only: 10 % for activities with the art. 68.4 deduction (LIRPF DA 64ª, RDL 22/2026).

Example (EDS, 2026): income 60,000; expenses 18,000 (including RETA 3,600). Difference 42,000; 5 % = 2,100 → capped at 2,000. Rendimiento neto 40,000.

## Reductions of art. 32

| Reduction | Conditions | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| 32.2.1º: TRADE / single-client workers | Estimación directa (EDS incompatible with the 5 % allowance); all sales to a single unrelated payer or TRADE status; expenses ≤ 30 % of income; no work income (except unemployment benefits ≤ 4,000); ≥ 70 % of income subject to withholding; not through an entity in atribución | 2,000 euros, plus: if net business income < 19,747.5 and other income ≤ 6,500: 6,498 (if ≤ 14,047.5) or 6,498 − 1.14 × (net − 14,047.5); plus 3,500 for disability (7,750 if ≥ 65 % or needing assistance) |
| 32.2.3º: low income | Not meeting 32.2.1º and total non-exempt income < 12,000 | 1,620 if income ≤ 8,000; 1,620 − 0.405 × (income − 8,000) between 8,000 and 12,000; combined with the art. 20 work reduction max 3,700 |
| Floor | The result cannot be negative | |

Figures as in LIRPF art. 32.2 after Ley 31/2022 (effect 2023).

## Start-up reduction 20 % (art. 32.3)

- Estimación directa only.
- 20 % of positive net income (after 32.1-32.2 reductions) in the **first year with positive net income and the next**; capped at 100,000 euros of net income per year.
- "Start" means no economic activity in the year before (activities stopped without ever having positive income do not count).
- Lost in a year in which more than 50 % of income comes from the person's employer of the year before.
- Independent of the 7 % withholding rule, which has its own test (no professional activity in the prior year).

## Irregular income 30 % (art. 32.1)

Income generated over more than 2 years, or classed as notoriously irregular (RIRPF art. 25: subsidies for fixed assets in some cases, compensation for lost activity, prizes, etc.), taxed in one year: 30 % reduction, on up to 300,000 euros. Not for activities that regularly produce such income (e.g. a lawyer's long cases).

## IRPF scale and the annual return

- General base: state scale 9.5 / 12 / 15 / 18.5 / 22.5 / 24.5 % (LIRPF art. 63, brackets to 12,450 / 20,200 / 35,200 / 60,000 / 300,000) plus the regional scale of the comunidad autónoma of residence. Combined top marginal rates range from about 45 % to 54 % depending on the region (unverified per region; check the regional law).
- Savings base (dividends, interest, gains): 19 / 21 / 23 / 27 / 30 % combined (brackets 6,000 / 50,000 / 200,000 / 300,000), the 30 % top rate from 2025 (Ley 7/2024).
- Personal allowance (*mínimo personal*) 5,550 euros (LIRPF art. 57); regions may change their part.
- An autónomo always files modelo 100 if they have business income (LIRPF art. 96 thresholds do not exempt them in practice when the activity income exceeds 1,000 euros plus other income; check art. 96.2-3).
- Campaign for 2025 income: 8 April - 30 June 2026 (Orden HAC/277/2026).

## Partner of an SL invoicing as a professional

LIRPF art. 27.1, third paragraph: income of a partner from services to an entity whose activity is professional (sección 2ª IAE), when the partner is in RETA or an alternative mutual, is business income (*rendimiento de actividad económica*): the partner invoices with IVA and suffers 15 % withholding. Otherwise it is work income. Market value: LIS art. 18.6 safe harbour (see `spanish-corporate-tax`, `references/operaciones-vinculadas.md`).
