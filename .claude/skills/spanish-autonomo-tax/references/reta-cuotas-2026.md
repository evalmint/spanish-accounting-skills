## How the system works

Since 1 January 2023 (RDL 13/2022), autónomos contribute on a base chosen from a table according to their **forecast** average monthly net income; the Social Security regularises the year once the AEAT reports the real income (LGSS art. 308). The tables were set for 2023-2025 in RDL 13/2022 DT 1ª. For 2026, with the 2023 budget still extended, RDL 3/2026 art. 3.4 (convalidated by Congress, Resolución 26 February 2026) keeps the **2025 tables**, updating only the maximum base of tramos 11 and 12 to the general ceiling. Orden PJC/297/2026, de 30 de marzo, art. 18, publishes them.

## Computing "rendimientos" for RETA

LGSS art. 308.1.c:

| Situation | Rendimiento computable |
|---|---|
| Estimación directa | IRPF net business income **plus** the RETA quotas (and alternative mutual contributions) of the owner |
| Módulos | Rendimiento neto previo (farming: minorado) |
| Partner/director of an SL in RETA (art. 305.2.b) | All work income from the company, plus all dividends and other income from own funds of companies where they hold ≥ 33 % or ≥ 25 % as director |
| Members of comunidades de bienes, civil partnerships (305.2.c-e) | Their share of the entity's income plus work/capital income from it |

Then subtract a generic-expenses deduction of **7 %** (3 % for societarios and art. 305.2.e partners with 90 days in RETA in the year). Divide by the months in RETA.

Example (2026): EDS net income 30,000 after deducting RETA quotas of 4,800. Computable: 34,800 × 0.93 = 32,364 /12 = 2,697/month → general tramo 7 (base 1,356.21 to 2,760). Minimum cuota 427.21/month.

## Choosing and changing the base

- Pick the tramo matching the forecast; any base within that tramo's min-max or, upwards, up to 5,101.20.
- Change it up to **6 times a year**, effective on the 1st of the month after the two-month period in which it is requested (requests in January-February take effect 1 March, and so on) (RD 2064/1995 art. 43 bis; exact effect dates unverified). Via Importass.
- Choosing a base above the minimum raises future benefits (sick pay, pension); it never reduces the regularisation risk upwards.

## Regularisation

LGSS art. 308.1.c, reglas 3ª-6ª:

| Outcome | Effect |
|---|---|
| Real income in the same tramo as the base chosen (and base inside its min-max) | No regularisation |
| Real income higher | TGSS claims the difference up to the minimum base of the real tramo; pay by the last day of the month after notification, **no surcharge or interest** if paid in time |
| Real income lower | TGSS refunds the excess over the maximum base of the real tramo, by 30 April of the year after the AEAT reports |
| No IRPF return / no data | Base mínima of tramo 1 of the general table is taken (art. 308.1.c regla 5ª) |

Timing: the AEAT sends the data after the IRPF campaign; 2023 regularisations started in 2025; 2024 regularisations are being notified in late 2025-2026 (unverified schedule). Keep a cash reserve when income grows quickly.

## Tarifa plana (cuota reducida)

LETA (Ley 20/2007) art. 38 ter, as rewritten by RDL 13/2022:

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Who | Initial registration in RETA, or no RETA registration in the previous 2 years (3 years if the reduction was used before); includes partners of capital companies and cooperatives in RETA (art. 38 ter.9); excludes relatives (colaboradores) to 2nd degree (38 ter.11) |
| First period | Reduced quota for the **12 full calendar months** after registration (plus the month of registration), covering CC and CP; exempt from cese and FP |
| Amount | **80 euros/month** for 2023-2025 (RDL 13/2022 DT 5ª); from 2026 it "shall be set by the PGE". With no 2026 PGE, the TGSS keeps charging 80 euros plus the MEI (reported effective quota about 88.6 euros in 2026) (unverified legal basis and exact amount) |
| Extension | 12 more months if the net income of that period is expected below the annual SMI; must be requested before the period starts, with a declaration |
| Disability ≥ 33 %, gender violence or terrorism victims | 24 months + 36 months; 80 euros for the first 24 months and 160 euros from month 25 (2023-2025 amounts, DT 5ª) |
| Benefits | Computed on the minimum base of tramo 1 of the general table |
| Regularisation | None in the first 12 months; in the extension, only if income exceeded the SMI |
| Loss | Deregistration during the period ends it |

Many regions add regional grants that pay the quota above the 80 euros or extend the 80 euros; ask the region.

## Autónomo societario and colaborador

| Case | Rule |
|---|---|
| SL partner with effective control who works in the company (≥ 25 % and director, or ≥ 33 % working, or ≥ 50 % with family): RETA | LGSS art. 305.2.b |
| Minimum base 2026 | Not below the general-regime group 7 minimum base: **1,424.40 euros/month** (LGSS art. 308.1.a regla 4ª; RDL 13/2022 DT 7ª says this rule applies from 2026; the 2023 figure was 1,000 euros). Orden PJC/297/2026 art. 18.4 lets them keep the 2025 provisional base during 2026, but the definitive base after regularisation cannot be below the regla 4ª minimum. Minimum cuota therefore about 448.69/month (1,424.40 × 31.5 %) |
| Generic deduction for regularisation | 3 % instead of 7 % |
| Colaborador (spouse, relatives to 2nd degree living with and working for the autónomo) | Same group 7 minimum base (regla 4ª); New colaboradores (spouse, partner, relatives to 2nd degree, not in RETA in the previous 5 years): 50 % bonificación for 24 months (LETA art. 35; base of the bonus as defined there) |

The societario's RETA quota is paid personally; if the company pays it, it is pay in kind for the partner and deductible for the company (DGT doctrine, unverified).

## Pluriactividad, mutualists and other cases

- Pluriactividad (employee and autónomo at once): refund of 50 % of the excess when total contributions exceed the threshold set yearly (2026 threshold updated by RDL 3/2026 art. 3.5; amount not checked here, unverified), requested automatically by the TGSS by 1 May of the next year.
- Alternative mutuals (lawyers, architects, etc.): professionals who chose the colegio's mutual instead of RETA. Ley 2/2026, de 29 de julio (BOE 31 July 2026, BOE-A-2026-16653), modifies LGSS DA 18ª-19ª: creates a voluntary, irreversible *pasarela* to RETA for colegiados who were in an alternative mutual before 1 August 2026, transferring their mutual savings in exchange for recognised RETA periods, with a special rule for those aged 52 or more on 31 December 2026; details depend on a regulation (details unverified; check the regulation and its application window).
- Over pensionable age and still working: *jubilación activa* rules (LGSS art. 214) and RETA solidarity contribution; out of scope here.
