## General test

An expense is deductible in IRPF estimación directa if it is (LIRPF art. 28.1 referring to LIS; RIRPF art. 22 for assets):

1. linked to the activity (*correlación con ingresos*);
2. accrued in the year and booked in the libros registro;
3. supported by a full invoice (*factura completa*) in the autónomo's name and NIF (a simplified ticket is generally not enough; RD 1619/2012);
4. not excluded (LIS art. 15 by reference: fines, the IRPF itself, gifts beyond 1 % of income, private spending).

Under módulos, none of this applies: the expense side is built into the modules (`modulos.md`).

## Home office

Conditions: the part of the home is declared as affected to the activity in the census declaration (modelo 036 (modelo 037 abolished from 3 February 2025, Orden HAC/1526/2024), casilla of "local afecto"), and is used for it.

| Cost | Computation |
|---|---|
| Rent of the whole home 1,000/month, 20 % of m² used | 200/month deductible. Whether the tenant must withhold 19 % on the affected part of a residential lease is a fact-specific question (unverified: check DGT doctrine for the case) |
| Owned home: IBI, community, insurance, mortgage interest, depreciation 3 % of building value | 20 % of each |
| Electricity 900/year, internet 600/year | 30 % × 20 % = 6 %: 54 + 36 = 90 deductible |

The 30 % rule is a presumption: the taxpayer may prove a different percentage (art. 30.2.5ª.b, "salvo que se pruebe un porcentaje superior o inferior"). A separate meter or dedicated business line allows 100 % of that bill. The AEAT does not accept the 30 % rule for a home that is not declared as affected.

## Vehicle

RIRPF art. 22.2: an asset used for both business and private needs is not affected, except for minor private use on non-working days. Cars (*automóviles de turismo*), their trailers, motorcycles, aircraft and boats are only affected if used exclusively, with the exceptions of art. 22.4:

- transport of goods, passengers for payment (taxis), driving schools;
- sales representatives' and agents' vehicles used for their travel;
- vehicles used for services of transport, of testing, of rental.

In practice, most consultants and professionals cannot deduct a private car in IRPF: the AEAT presumes some private use. A van (*furgoneta*, vehículo mixto) used for the trade is usually accepted. The IVA rule is different: 50 % presumption (see below).

## Meals and travel

- Meals of the autónomo (since 2018, LIRPF art. 30.2.5ª.c): in restaurants and similar, paid electronically (card, transfer), in the course of the activity, with the RIRPF art. 9 daily limits. Lunches at home or supermarket shopping are not deductible.
- Travel and lodging: deductible when justified; there is no fixed daily cap for hotels or transport for autónomos.
- Meals with clients count as client entertainment (1 % limit), not as the owner's manutención.

## Health insurance and mutual funds

- Health insurance premiums (owner, spouse, children under 25 living at home): 500 euros per person per year, 1,500 if disabled (LIRPF art. 30.2.5ª.a).
- Alternative mutual of a professional not in RETA: deductible as an expense up to the maximum contingencias comunes quota of the RETA in that year (art. 30.2.1ª); the excess can go as a pension contribution reduction (LIRPF art. 51).

## Family members

- Spouse or minor children working in the activity: salary deductible if there is a labour contract and Social Security registration, at market value (LIRPF art. 30.2.2ª). Often the spouse is instead an *autónomo colaborador* in RETA; whether the owner may deduct a colaborador's quota that the owner pays depends on DGT doctrine (unverified).
- Assets ceded by spouse or minor children (e.g. premises): rent at market value is deductible for the owner and capital income for them (art. 30.2.3ª), not for jointly-owned spousal assets.

## Assets and depreciation

| Regime | Rule |
|---|---|
| EDS | Simplified table (Orden de 27 de marzo de 1998): e.g. buildings 3 % (68 years), furniture and installations 10 % (20 years), machinery 12 % (18 years), computers and software 26 % (10 years), transport elements 16 % (14 years), tools 30 % (8 years) (AEAT Manual Renta 2025 reproduces the table; the computer row is confirmed, the others should be checked against the Orden's Anexo); ERD incentives apply |
| EDN | IS table (LIS art. 12.1) |
| Both | New items ≤ 300 euros each: free depreciation up to 25,000 euros a year (LIS art. 12.3.e); ERD double rate on new assets (LIS art. 103) because the autónomo is treated like an ERD if INCN < 10 M (LIRPF art. 30 referring to LIS ch. XI; RIRPF art. 30.1ª for EDS) |
| Affectation | Assets used partly for private purposes are not affected (RIRPF art. 22.2), except divisible assets (part of a building) |

## IVA on the same expenses

| Expense | IVA deduction | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Car | Presumed 50 % affected; 100 % only for listed uses (transport of goods, taxis, driving schools, sales reps, security) | LIVA art. 95.Tres.2º |
| Home suministros | Proportion of affectation: the AEAT and the DGT accept the same proportion as IRPF (V2370-18 line) (unverified consulta number) | LIVA art. 95 |
| Meals, entertainment, gifts to clients | Not deductible (except samples and gifts ≤ 200 euros/year per recipient in some cases) | LIVA art. 96.Uno.5º-6º |
| Jewellery, food and drink, leisure | Not deductible | LIVA art. 96 |
