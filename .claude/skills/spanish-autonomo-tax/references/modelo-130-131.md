## Modelo 130 (estimación directa)

| Element | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Autónomos in estimación directa (normal or simplificada) | RIRPF art. 109.1 |
| Exempt | Professionals with at least **70 %** of the previous year's activity income subject to withholding; same test for farming and forestry; in the start year, the quarter's percentage counts | RIRPF art. 109.2-5 |
| Amount | **20 %** of year-to-date net income (1 January to quarter-end), minus payments of earlier quarters, minus year-to-date withholdings suffered (professionals and others in art. 110.3.a) | RIRPF art. 110.1.a, 110.3 |
| Low-income reduction | If prior-year net activity income ≤ 12,000: deduct 100 (≤ 9,000), 75 (9,000-10,000), 50 (10,000-11,000), 25 (11,000-12,000) per quarter | RIRPF art. 110.3.c |
| Mortgage on main home (pre-2013 regime) | 2 % of net income deduction, max 660.14 per quarter (unverified figure) | RIRPF art. 110.3.d |
| Ceuta/Melilla | Percentages reduced by 60 % | RIRPF art. 110.2 |
| Negative result | Return filed with zero to pay ("negativa"); the negative amount offsets later quarters of the same year | |
| Deadlines | 1-20 April, July, October; 1-30 January (4th quarter) | RIRPF art. 111 |

Example (EDS, 2026): Q1 income 15,000, expenses 6,000 → net 9,000 (the 5 % allowance may be applied quarterly); 20 % = 1,800; no withholdings → pay 1,800. Q2 cumulative net 20,000: 4,000 − 1,800 = 2,200.

If most clients are businesses and withhold 15 %, the 70 % test usually exempts the professional from modelo 130; the annual modelo 100 settles the difference.

## Modelo 131 (módulos)

4 % of the módulos net income computed with the data on 1 January (3 % with one employee, 2 % with none; 2 % of quarterly income for farming), minus the quarter's withholdings (1 % withholding by business clients for certain módulos activities, RIRPF art. 95.6), same deadlines (RIRPF arts. 110.1.b-c, 111). See `modulos.md`.

## Withholdings on the autónomo's invoices

| Income | Withholding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Professional activities (IAE sección 2ª and 3ª), invoiced to businesses or professionals | **15 %** of the base (before IVA) | RIRPF art. 95.1 |
| New professionals: start year and next 2, no professional activity in the previous year, after notifying the client in writing | **7 %** | RIRPF art. 95.1 |
| Recaudadores, insurance mediators with auxiliaries, lottery delegates, and some artists with low income | 7 % | RIRPF art. 95.1 |
| Professional income entitled to the Ceuta/Melilla deduction | Reduced by 60 % | RIRPF art. 95.1 |
| Módulos activities listed (e.g. some manufacturing, transport, builders' trades) invoiced to businesses | 1 % | RIRPF art. 95.6 |
| Farming and livestock | 2 % (1 % pig and poultry fattening) | RIRPF art. 95.4 |
| Business (sección 1ª) activities in estimación directa | None | |
| Invoices to consumers | None | RIRPF art. 76 (only businesses withhold) |

Invoice example: base 1,000; IVA 21 % 210; IRPF withholding 15 % −150; total to pay 1,060. Client books **4751** for the withholding and pays it with modelo 111.

## Libros registro

RIRPF art. 68:

| Situation | Books |
|---|---|
| Business activity (commercial) in EDN | Accounting under the Código de Comercio (diario, inventarios y cuentas anuales) |
| Business activity in EDS, or non-commercial | Libro registro de ventas e ingresos; de compras y gastos; de bienes de inversión |
| Professional activity (EDN or EDS) | Libro registro de ingresos; de gastos; de bienes de inversión; de provisiones de fondos y suplidos |
| Módulos | Keep numbered invoices received; libro de bienes de inversión if depreciation is deducted; libro de ventas e ingresos where income determines the index (e.g. certain farming); IVA simplificado: libro registro de facturas recibidas |

The AEAT publishes a common format for IRPF and IVA libros (Orden HAC/773/2019 and successive). Books are not legalised at the Registro Mercantil (unless commercial accounting is voluntarily kept). Retain for 4 years (LGT art. 66) and as long as relevant for BIN-like items. From 1 July 2027, the invoicing software must meet Verifactu (RD 1007/2023 as amended by RDL 15/2025; 1 January 2027 for IS taxpayers): see `spanish-invoice-compliance`.

## No IVA franquicia in Spain

Council Directive (EU) 2020/285 lets Member States exempt small businesses (domestic turnover up to 85,000 euros; cross-border SME scheme from 1 January 2025). Spain has **not** introduced a domestic franchise: the LIVA consolidated text (last update 07/10/2026) has no franchise regime, and every autónomo charges IVA from the first euro unless an exemption applies. The cross-border SME scheme (Directive art. 284) is opted for in the Member State of establishment and relies on the domestic franchise there; whether a Spanish business can use it without a Spanish domestic scheme has not been confirmed (unverified). Any agent seeing "no IVA under 85,000" from another country must not apply it to Spain.
