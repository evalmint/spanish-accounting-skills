## Rates in force, by period start

Ley 7/2024 (BOE 21/12/2024, disposición final 8ª) rewrote LIS art. 29.1 with effect for periods starting on or after 1 January 2025 and added DT 44ª with the phase-in. The definitive figures sit in art. 29.1; DT 44ª overrides them for 2025-2028.

| Period starting in | Micro (INCN < 1 M): 0-50,000 | Micro: rest | ERD (art. 101, INCN < 10 M) | General | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 and earlier | 23 % (INCN < 1 M, flat) | 23 % | 25 % | 25 % | art. 29.1 as amended by Ley 31/2022 |
| 2025 | 21 % | 22 % | 24 % | 25 % | DT 44ª.1 |
| 2026 | 19 % | 21 % | 23 % | 25 % | DT 44ª.2 |
| 2027 | 17 % | 20 % | 22 % | 25 % | art. 29.1 (micro); DT 44ª.3 (ERD) |
| 2028 | 17 % | 20 % | 21 % | 25 % | art. 29.1; DT 44ª.4 |
| 2029 on | 17 % | 20 % | 20 % | 25 % | art. 29.1 |

- Periods shorter than a year: the 50,000-euro first bracket is prorated by days/365 (art. 29.1; DT 44ª).
- *Empresas emergentes* certified under Ley 28/2022 (start-up law): 15 % in the first period with a positive base and the next three, as long as they keep the status (Ley 28/2022 art. 7, wording not rechecked against BOE: unverified; LIS art. 29.1 only gives 2 periods to ordinary new entities).
- Other rates (art. 29): new entities 15 %; cooperatives fiscalmente protegidas 3 points less (max 20 %); Ley 49/2002 entities 10 %; SICAV and FII 1 %; pension funds 0 %; credit institutions and hydrocarbons 30 %; ZEC entities special rate (art. 29.7, Ley 19/1994 art. 43).

## Which bucket: the INCN tests

| Test | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Microempresa | INCN of the **immediately preceding period** < 1,000,000 euros | art. 29.1 |
| ERD | INCN of the preceding period < 10,000,000 euros; not an entidad patrimonial | art. 101.1 |
| Group aggregation | INCN is that of the whole group (Código de Comercio art. 42), and also of companies controlled by one individual with spouse and relatives to 2nd degree | arts. 29.1 and 101.3 |
| Annualising | Prior period shorter than a year, or activity for less than a year: INCN is grossed up to 12 months | art. 101.2 |
| New company | INCN of the first period in which the activity is actually carried on | art. 101.2 |
| ERD grace | Keeps ERD incentives for the 3 periods after first reaching 10 M, if it was ERD in that period and the 2 before | art. 101.4 |

Note the different look-back windows elsewhere: *tributación mínima*, DA 15ª (BIN limits for large companies), DA 14ª (23 % minimum instalment) and art. 40.3 (mandatory method) use the INCN of the **12 months before the period starts**, not the previous period.

The ERD grace (art. 101.4) gives the ERD incentives of arts. 102-105. Whether it also gives the ERD *rate* is read from art. 29.1 ("entidades que cumplan las previsiones previstas en el artículo 101"), which suggests yes (unverified against AEAT doctrine).

## Entidad de nueva creación

15 % in the first period with a positive base and in the following one (art. 29.1, para. 5). Not new if:

1. the activity was carried on before by a related party (art. 18) and transferred to the company;
2. the activity was carried on in the year before incorporation by an individual holding more than 50 % of the new company;
3. the company belongs to a group under Código de Comercio art. 42 (regardless of residence or consolidation);
4. it is an entidad patrimonial.

Interaction: if a lower rate applies (micro bracket 17 % vs 15 %, no; but a cooperative rate, yes), the lower rate wins. A new company is also exempt from the 70 % BIN limit in its first 3 periods with a positive base (art. 26.3) and uses 10 % instead of 15 % for tributación mínima if it reaches that regime (art. 30 bis.1).

## Entidad patrimonial

LIS art. 5.2: more than half of the assets are shares or are not used in an economic activity, measured on the average of the quarterly balance sheets. Consequences: 25 % rate (no micro, ERD or new-entity rate), no ERD incentives (art. 101.1), BIN restrictions on change of control (art. 26.4.c.3º), no reserva de nivelación. A property-letting business counts as an activity only with at least one employee on a full-time labour contract (art. 5.1).

## From resultado contable to cuota diferencial

Mirrors the modelo 200 page structure.

| Step | Item | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Resultado contable antes de impuestos (PyG before **630**) | art. 10.3 |
| 2 | ± Ajustes extracontables: permanent and temporary differences | arts. 11-23; `ajustes-extracontables.md` |
| 3 | = Base imponible previa | |
| 4 | − Reserva de capitalización (limit 20 %/25 % of step 3 before 11.12 and BINs) | art. 25 |
| 5 | − Compensación de BINs (70 % of base before step 4 and BINs; always up to 1 M) | art. 26 |
| 6 | = Base imponible; − reserva de nivelación (ERD) | art. 105 |
| 7 | × Tipo de gravamen = cuota íntegra | arts. 29-30 |
| 8 | − Bonificaciones (Ceuta/Melilla art. 33, local public services art. 34) | |
| 9 | − Double-taxation deductions (arts. 31-32) | |
| 10 | − Incentive deductions (I+D+i, cinema, employment of disabled workers, etc.), with limits | arts. 35-39 |
| 11 | = Cuota líquida (never negative; not below cuota líquida mínima if art. 30 bis applies) | arts. 30, 30 bis |
| 12 | − Retenciones and ingresos a cuenta (**473**) − pagos fraccionados (modelo 202) | art. 41 |
| 13 | = Cuota diferencial: positive is paid with modelo 200; negative is refunded de oficio | arts. 41, 127 |

The *devengo* (tax point) is the last day of the tax period (art. 28); the period equals the financial year and never exceeds 12 months (art. 27).
