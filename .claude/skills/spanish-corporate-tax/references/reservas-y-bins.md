## Reserva de capitalización (art. 25)

Reduction of the base for retaining profits. Rules for periods starting from 1 January 2025 (Ley 7/2024 DF 8ª.2):

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Who | Taxpayers at the art. 29.1 rates (general, micro, ERD, new entity) or 29.6 (30 %) |
| Reduction | **20 %** of the increase in own funds (10 % before 2024; the 2024 wording from RDL 4/2024 art. 4.2 also differs (unverified percentage): check the BOE "redacción" selector if a 2024 return is in play) |
| Headcount bonus | 23 % if average headcount grows 2-5 %; 26.5 % if 5-10 %; 30 % if more than 10 %; the increase must be kept 3 years |
| Cap | 20 % of the base before this reduction, before art. 11.12 and before BINs; **25 %** if INCN of the previous 12 months < 1 M euros |
| Carry-forward | Unused amount for lack of base: applied in the periods ending in the next 2 years, inside the same cap |
| Holding period | The own-funds increase must be kept **3 years** (5 years before 2024; DT 43ª applies the 3 years to reserves still running in 2024), unless there are accounting losses |
| Reserve | A restricted reserve (*indisponible*) for the reduction amount, shown separately in the balance sheet, for 3 years |
| Increase measured | Own funds at year-end without that year's result, minus own funds at start without the prior year's result |
| Excluded from both ends | Partner contributions, capital increases by debt conversion, own-share and restructuring movements, legal and statutory reserves, nivelación and RIC reserves, compound instruments, DTA changes from rate changes |
| Breach | Regularise with interest in the period of breach (art. 125.3) |

Practical consequences:

- The increase is essentially the prior year's profit kept as voluntary reserves (**113**) minus any distributions charged to old reserves. Allocating 10 % to the legal reserve (**112**) does not count.
- The reserve is booked in the year after (when the *junta* allocates the result); a separate subaccount of **114** with title "Reserva de capitalización Ley 27/2014" is common practice. No PGC account is specific to it; company subaccounts under **113** or **114** are used.
- The legal reserve does not count as the reserva de capitalización: art. 25.1.b asks for a reserve "with absolute separation and its own title", and art. 25.2.d keeps legal reserves out of the computation. Book a distinct reserve.

Example (2026, ERD, base before reductions 200,000, own funds grew by 120,000 from retained 2025 profit, headcount flat): reduction 20 % × 120,000 = 24,000; cap 20 % × 200,000 = 40,000; apply 24,000. At 23 %, saving 5,520. Reserve of 24,000 booked out of 2026 profit by mid-2027 and kept until 2029 year-end.

## Reserva de nivelación (art. 105)

Tax deferral for ERDs, not a permanent saving.

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Who | Entities meeting art. 101 (ERD) in the period that "apply the rate of art. 29.1, first paragraph" |
| Reduction | Up to **10 %** of the positive base, max **1 M euros** (prorated for short periods) |
| Reversal | Added back to the base when a negative base arises in the next **5 years**, up to that negative base; whatever is left is added back in year 5 |
| Reserve | Restricted reserve for the amount, charged to that year's profit (or the first later profits) |
| Compatibility | The same profits cannot fund both nivelación and capitalización reserves (art. 105.5), but both reductions can be applied in the same year with separate reserves |
| Breach | Add the tax on the reduced amount, increased by 5 %, plus interest (art. 105.6) |
| Instalments | The reduction is taken into account in art. 40.3 instalments (art. 105.4) |

Open point: art. 105 (unchanged since 2015) refers to "the rate of art. 29.1, first paragraph". Since Ley 7/2024 the ERD rate sits in a later paragraph of art. 29.1. The AEAT Manual de Sociedades 2025 still presents nivelación as an ERD incentive; whether an ERD paying 24 %/23 % (DT 44ª) qualifies has not been confirmed by a published DGT ruling (unverified). Ask the user's adviser before relying on it in 2025-2028.

Value: at 23 %, 10 % of a 300,000 base defers 6,900 of tax for up to 5 years. It works as loss relief taken in advance: if a loss arrives, the reserved amount absorbs it instead of creating a BIN. If no loss arrives, the tax is simply paid in year 5 (at that year's rate, which for an ERD will be lower: 20 % from 2029). Low value when the company never makes losses; useful when a loss is likely.

In the ERD grace years (art. 101.4) nivelación stays available, since it is one of the "incentivos fiscales establecidos en este capítulo".

## Compensación de bases imponibles negativas (art. 26)

| Element | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Time limit | None: BINs carry forward indefinitely | art. 26.1 |
| General limit | 70 % of the base before reserva de capitalización and before BINs | art. 26.1 |
| Floor | Up to 1 M euros a year always offsettable (prorated for short periods) | art. 26.1-2 |
| INCN 20-60 M (12 months before) | 50 % | DA 15ª (re-enacted by Ley 7/2024 for periods from 2024 after STC 11/2024 annulled the RDL 3/2016 version) |
| INCN ≥ 60 M | 25 % | DA 15ª |
| New entity | No 70 % limit in the first 3 periods with a positive base | art. 26.3 |
| Extinction | No limit in the period in which the company is wound up (unless restructuring) | art. 26.1 |
| Debt forgiveness | No limit on income from *quitas o esperas* agreed with creditors | art. 26.1 |
| Change of control | No offset if > 50 % acquired by parties holding < 25 % at the loss year-end and the company is dormant, changes activity, or is patrimonial | art. 26.4 |
| Proof | AEAT may check BINs for 10 years from the filing deadline of the loss year; afterwards the taxpayer proves them with the return and the accounts deposited at the Registro Mercantil | art. 26.5 |

Example (2026, micro, base before reductions 1,500,000 in a company with 2,000,000 of BINs): limit max(70 % × 1,500,000 = 1,050,000; 1,000,000) = 1,050,000 offset; base 450,000.

Typical SME: base under 1 M euros means BINs can wipe out the whole base. Order matters: the capitalización cap is computed **before** BINs, and the 70 % is computed **before** capitalización.

## Exención por doble imposición (art. 21)

Dividends and capital gains on shares are exempt when:

| Requirement | Rule |
|---|---|
| Stake | At least **5 %** direct or indirect (the alternative 20 M euro acquisition-value test was removed from 2021; DT 40ª keeps it for holdings bought before 2021 until 2025) |
| Holding period | One year without interruption at the distribution date (can be completed afterwards); for gains, at the transfer date |
| Non-resident subsidiary | Subject to a foreign tax analogous to IS at a nominal rate of at least **10 %** (treaty with information exchange = presumed) |
| Holding companies | If more than 70 % of the subsidiary's income is dividends or gains, look-through to indirect holdings |
| Management-cost haircut | Exempt amount reduced by **5 %** (art. 21.10): effectively 95 % exemption. At 25 % the tax cost is 1.25 % of the dividend |
| Small-parent exception | Recipient with INCN < 40 M in the previous period, not patrimonial, not in a group before setting up the subsidiary, receiving dividends from a subsidiary created after 1 January 2021: no 5 % haircut for 3 years (art. 21.11) |

Book: dividend income in **760**; negative adjustment of 95 % in the return; no deferred tax (permanent).

## Order of application

1. Base imponible previa (resultado + ajustes, including art. 21).
2. Reserva de capitalización (cap on step 1).
3. BINs (70 % / 1 M computed on step 1, before step 2).
4. Reserva de nivelación (10 % of the positive base after BINs).
5. Rate.
