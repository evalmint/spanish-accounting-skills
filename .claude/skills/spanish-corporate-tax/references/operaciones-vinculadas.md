## Who is related (art. 18.2)

| Pair | Threshold |

| Company and its directors (de jure and de facto) | Always, except for the pay for acting as director |
| Company and spouses or relatives to 3rd degree of partners (≥ 25 %) or directors | |

## Market value and methods

Related transactions are valued at market value: what independent parties would agree (art. 18.1). Methods (art. 18.4, RIS art. 17): comparable uncontrolled price, cost plus, resale price, profit split, transactional net margin; others if those cannot be applied. The taxpayer, not the AEAT, picks the most suitable one.

The AEAT corrects the value only if the agreed value differs and the difference matters for tax (art. 18.10-11): it then applies the correction to both parties (bilateral adjustment), and the difference is recharacterised (secondary adjustment).

## Socio-profesional safe harbour (art. 18.6)

For services by a professional partner (individual) to a professional company, the agreed pay is deemed market value if all hold:

1. More than **75 %** of the company's income comes from professional activities, and it has adequate human and material means.
2. Total pay to all professional partners ≥ **75 %** of the result before that pay.
3. Each partner's pay is set by written qualitative/quantitative criteria of contribution, and is at least **1.5 ×** the average salary of employees doing similar functions or, if none, at least **5 × IPREM** (IPREM 2026: unverified; 2025 annual IPREM with 14 payments was 8,400 euros, so 5 × = 42,000).

Typical use: lawyers', consultants', architects' and doctors' SLs where the partner bills through the company. Failing the safe harbour does not make the pay wrong; it removes the presumption.

## Administrator and partner pay

The tax treatment depends on which hat the person wears:

| Situation | Company side (IS) | Individual side (IRPF) | Social Security |
|---|---|---|---|
| Director pay for the post (*cargo de administrador*), bylaws say it is paid and set the system (LSC art. 217), amount approved by the junta | Deductible (not a liberalidad) | *Rendimiento del trabajo*; withholding 35 % (19 % if company INCN < 100,000) (RIRPF art. 80.1.3º) | RETA if control (LGSS art. 305.2.b) |
| Director pay with gratuitous bylaws, or without junta approval | Risk: non-deductible (TS 2 January 2018 "Mecanotubo" line; unverified citation) | Still taxable | |
| Partner who works in the company, professional services of the type the company sells, partner registered in RETA or a mutual | Deductible at market value (art. 18) | *Rendimiento de actividad económica* (LIRPF art. 27.1, third para.): invoice with VAT and 15 %/7 % withholding | RETA |
| Partner with other functions (management) | Deductible if contracted | *Rendimiento del trabajo* | RETA if control ≥ 25 % + director or ≥ 33 % (LGSS art. 305.2.b) |
| Dividends | Not deductible (art. 15.a) | *Rendimiento del capital mobiliario*, savings base 19-30 % (LIRPF art. 66, Ley 7/2024 top rate 30 % from 2025 above 300,000) | n/a |

DGT consultations since 2019 treat the senior-management functions of a director as absorbed into the post of director (*teoría del vínculo*): their pay is director pay, taxed as work income, and needs the bylaws clause to be safely deductible (unverified as to the exact consulta numbers).

## Typical owner-company transactions

| Transaction | Risk | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Partner lends money to the company | Interest must be at market; interest-free loan is accepted in practice for the lender individual (DGT position that no imputed interest arises for an individual lender when there is no consideration) (unverified) | Loan contract, rate, account **551** or **1634**/**5134** if long/short-term partner loan |
| Company lends money to partner | Interest at market must be charged and is income for the company; a loan never repaid can be recast as a dividend | Contract, interest, repayment schedule; **551** balances that grow every year are an audit trigger |
| Company pays partner's private expenses | Non-deductible (art. 15.e) and IRPF income in kind for the partner | Separate private spending, reclassify to **551** |
| Company car used by partner | IRPF income in kind (20 % of new-car price a year, reduced for efficient cars, LIRPF art. 43.1.1º.b) and IVA deduction limits | Usage records |
| Partner rents premises to the company | Rent at market; company withholds 19 % (modelo 115) | Lease contract, cadastral value |
| Sale of assets between partner and company | Market value both sides; a gain may arise for the seller | Valuation |

## Documentation (art. 18.3, RIS arts. 13-16)

| Case | Obligation |
|---|---|
| Transactions with the same related party ≤ 250,000 euros in the year (market value) | No specific documentation (art. 18.3.d), still market value |
| Group INCN < 45 M | Simplified content (art. 18.3; RIS art. 16): describe the transaction, method and comparables |
| Group INCN ≥ 750 M | Country-by-country report (modelo 231) and master file |
| Exclusion of simplified regime | Transactions by módulos taxpayers with their ≥ 25 % companies, transfers of businesses, of shares, of real estate, of intangibles: full documentation regardless of size |

## Modelo 232

Informative return on related-party transactions and tax-haven transactions (RIS art. 13.4; Orden HFP/816/2017).

| Who reports | Transactions over the year with the same related party above **250,000 euros** (market value); or **specific transactions** above **100,000 euros** (in aggregate per type, e.g. sales of businesses, shares, real estate, intangibles, módulos taxpayers); or same-type transactions using the same method above 50 % of the company's INCN; or any amount with tax havens |
|---|---|
| Not reported | Transactions within a tax consolidation group; acceptances of public offers |
| Deadline | Month after the 10 months following year-end: for a calendar year, **1-30 November** of the next year (2025 year: 1-30 November 2026) |
| Form | Electronic only |

Example: SL with one partner-director who invoices the SL 60,000 a year and lends it 300,000: the loan principal is not "contraprestación"; interest of 12,000 plus services of 60,000 = 72,000 < 250,000, so no modelo 232 for the year (but market value still applies). Check the exact computation of loan transactions in the modelo 232 instructions.

## Secondary adjustment and penalties

- Secondary adjustment (art. 18.11): an overpayment by the company to a partner becomes a dividend (or a contribution if the reverse); restitution avoids it (RIS art. 20).
- Penalties for documentation failures (art. 18.13): 1,000 euros per item or 10,000 per set of data omitted or false, capped at 10 % of the related-party transactions or 1 % of INCN; if the AEAT also adjusts the value, 15 % of the adjustment. No penalty if the documentation is correct and the value adjusted was the one declared.
