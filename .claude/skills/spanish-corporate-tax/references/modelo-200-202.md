## Modelo 202: pagos fraccionados (art. 40)

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Periods | First 20 calendar days of **April, October and December**, on account of the period in progress on the 1st of each month (art. 40.1) |
| Who | Every IS taxpayer, except SICAV/FII (1 %) and pension funds (art. 40.1) |
| Nil returns | Under art. 40.2, if the base is zero nothing is filed; under art. 40.3 the return is filed even if zero (Orden HFP/227/2017 instructions; unverified for the current order) |
| Method 1: art. 40.2 | **18 %** of the last cuota íntegra whose filing deadline had passed on the first day of the payment window, minus deductions, bonificaciones, withholdings of that period. April and October use year n-2 or n-1 depending on whether the July filing has passed: April 2026 uses the 2024 return; October and December 2026 use the 2025 return |
| Method 2: art. 40.3 | Percentage × base imponible of the first 3, 9 or 11 months of the year, minus bonificaciones, withholdings of the period and prior instalments of the same period |
| Choice of method 2 | Census declaration (modelo 036) in **February** of the year it should apply, for calendar-year companies; binding until renounced in the same window |
| Mandatory method 2 | INCN > **6 M euros** in the 12 months before the period starts |
| Payment | Direct debit or NRC; an instalment is *deuda tributaria* (art. 40.5) and cannot be deferred (LGT art. 65.2.b) |

When to opt for method 2: the current year will be much worse than the reference year (method 1 would overpay), or the company is new (method 1 gives zero: no prior cuota). Method 1 suits stable or growing companies: it finances growth because instalments lag profits.

## Percentages for art. 40.3, by year

Percentage = **5/7 × rate, rounded down** to a whole number (art. 40.3), or **19/20 × rate, rounded up** for INCN ≥ 10 M (DA 14ª.1.b). With a two-bracket scale, each bracket gets its own percentage (modelo 202 instructions, casillas 21/24/62/65).

| Rate type | Rate 2025 | % 2025 | Rate 2026 | % 2026 | Rate 2027 | % 2027 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Micro, first 50,000 | 21 % | 15 % | 19 % | 13 % | 17 % | 12 % |
| Micro, rest | 22 % | 15 % | 21 % | 15 % | 20 % | 14 % |
| ERD | 24 % | 17 % | 23 % | 16 % | 22 % | 15 % |
| General, INCN < 10 M (non-ERD case is rare: e.g. patrimonial) | 25 % | 17 % | 25 % | 17 % | 25 % | 17 % |
| General, INCN ≥ 10 M | 25 % | **24 %** | 25 % | 24 % | 25 % | 24 % |
| New entity | 15 % | 10 % | 15 % | 10 % | 15 % | 10 % |
| Credit institutions, hydrocarbons, INCN ≥ 10 M | 30 % | 29 % | 30 % | 29 % | 30 % | 29 % |

For 2028 the ERD rate 21 % gives 15 %; for 2029 on, 20 % gives 14 %. These percentages are derived from the statutory formula; the AEAT modelo 202 instructions confirm the formula but the AEAT manual does not print the per-year table (derived, not quoted).

Example (ERD, 2026, method 2, October instalment): base Jan-Sep 150,000 × 16 % = 24,000, minus withholdings Jan-Sep 1,000, minus April instalment 4,000 = 19,000 to pay.

## Large companies: 23 % minimum (DA 14ª)

For INCN ≥ **10 M euros** in the 12 months before the period starts, the art. 40.3 instalment can never be less than **23 %** of the positive accounting result (PyG) of the 3, 9 or 11 months, minus prior instalments (25 % for 30 %-rate entities). Excluded from the result: income from debt forgiveness and debt-for-equity swaps not taxed under art. 17.2. Not applicable to the 10 %, 1 %, 0 % entities, SOCIMI and venture capital entities. The original RDL 2/2016 version was annulled by STC 11/2024 for 2016-2017; the regime in force rests on Ley 6/2018 (from 2018).

## Modelo 200: annual return

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Deadline | 25 calendar days after the 6 months following year-end (art. 124.1). Calendar year: 1-25 July; 2025 returns: until **27 July 2026** (25 July was a Saturday). Direct debit until 22 July 2026 (unverified for the debit cut-off) |
| Non-calendar year | e.g. year-end 30 September 2026: 1-25 April 2027 |
| 2025 form | Approved by Orden HAC/529/2026, de 7 de mayo (BOE-A-2026-11583) |
| Who | Every IS taxpayer, including dormant companies with no activity, and partially exempt entities (art. 124) |
| Filing | Electronic only; companies also may use the *Sociedades WEB* service, which imports the accounts deposited in the Registro Mercantil |
| Result to pay | Paid at filing (or direct debit); can be deferred/instalments under LGT art. 65 (not if withheld amounts) |
| Refund | Negative cuota diferencial: refunded de oficio within 6 months of the deadline, then interest (art. 127) |
| Corrections | In favour or against: *autoliquidación rectificativa* on the same form (RGAT as amended by RD 117/2024; check the order of the year (unverified for IS)); late payment without request: complementary/rectificativa with recargos LGT art. 27 (1 % + 1 % per month to 12 months, then 15 % + interest) |
| Foral companies paying to several administrations | Proportion of operations; separate forms (Bizkaia 200, Navarra S90) |

## Retenciones soportadas

Withholdings suffered by the company are payments on account of its IS (art. 41) and booked in **473**:

| Income received | Withholding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Interest, dividends, other capital income | 19 % | LIS art. 128.6.a |
| Rent of urban premises (company as landlord) | 19 % (modelo 115 by the tenant) | RIS art. 60 |
| Professional services invoiced by a company | None: companies do not suffer professional withholding (only individuals) | RIRPF art. 95 applies to IRPF taxpayers |
| Image rights | 24 % | LIS art. 128.6.b |
| Lottery prizes over the exempt amount | 20 % | LIS art. 128.6.c |

Exempt from withholding: dividends qualifying for art. 21 between Spanish companies (RIS art. 61.s), intra-group interest within a tax group, interest on bank deposits of credit institutions, etc. (RIS art. 61).

## Deducciones and their limits (arts. 35-39)

| Deduction | Rate | Source |
|---|---|---|
| I+D: expenses | 25 % of the year's expenses; 42 % on the excess over the average of the 2 prior years; +17 % on staff costs of qualified researchers exclusively in I+D | art. 35.1.c.1º |
| I+D: investments in tangible/intangible assets (not buildings) used only in I+D | 8 % | art. 35.1.c.2º |
| Innovación tecnológica (IT) | 12 %; **15 %** in the first 2 periods with IT spending, in periods where spending grows ≥ 20 % on the prior period, and where it is not below the average of the 2 prior periods | art. 35.2.c as amended by **RDL 40/2026** (in force 21 November 2026) |
| Spanish cinema and audiovisual productions | 30 % of the first 1 M of base, 25 % above | art. 36.1 |
| Employment of workers with disability | 9,000/12,000 euros per person-year of increase | art. 38 |

General limit (art. 39.1): incentive deductions applied in a year cannot exceed **25 %** of cuota íntegra minus double-taxation deductions and bonificaciones; **50 %** if I+D+i of the year exceeds 10 % of that cuota. Unused credit: 15 years; I+D+i 18 years.

I+D+i monetisation (art. 39.2, rewritten by RDL 40/2026): companies at the 29.1 or 29.6 rates may take I+D+i credits outside the limit with a 20 % discount, and claim them in cash if the cuota is insufficient, up to **3 M euros a year jointly per group** (+2 M for I+D when I+D spending > 10 % of INCN). Conditions: one year since the period ends without applying the credit; headcount (total or I+D) not reduced; 80 % of the amount reinvested in I+D+i within 24 months; *informe motivado* or *acuerdo previo de valoración*. Before RDL 40/2026 the caps were 1 M for IT and 3 M for I+D with the same 20 % discount (unverified wording of the previous caps). RDL 40/2026 entered into force on 21 November 2026 (DF 3ª) and its art. 33 carries no clause on which tax periods it affects, so application to periods in progress at that date is unverified. It must be ratified (convalidado) by Congress within 30 working days of 20 November 2026; three tax RDLs were repealed this way in 2025-2026, so recheck before relying on the 15 % or the new caps.

Cross-reference: software-company I+D+i qualification and *informe motivado* → `spanish-industry-saas`.

## Tributación mínima 15 % (art. 30 bis)

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Who | INCN ≥ **20 M euros** in the 12 months before the period starts, or any company in a tax consolidation group |
| Floor | Cuota líquida ≥ 15 % of the base imponible (after nivelación and RIC) |
| Reduced floors | New entities at 15 %: 10 %; 30 %-rate entities: 18 %; from 2025, micro and ERD scales: rate × 15/25 rounded up (art. 30 bis.1 as amended by Ley 7/2024): ERD 2026 23 % × 15/25 = 13.8 % → 14 % |
| Order | Bonificaciones and double-tax deductions first; incentive deductions only up to the floor; the excess carries forward |
| Not applicable | 10 %, 1 %, 0 % entities, SOCIMI |

Small companies (INCN < 20 M, not consolidating) are outside art. 30 bis.

## Pillar 2: Impuesto Complementario

Ley 7/2024, art. 1, transposes Directive (EU) 2022/2523: a top-up tax so that groups with consolidated revenue of at least **750 M euros** in at least two of the four preceding years pay an effective 15 % in each jurisdiction (domestic, primary and secondary top-up taxes), for periods starting from 31 December 2023 (secondary top-up from 31 December 2024). Small and mid-sized companies are outside it. Returns and forms: specific self-assessment and information returns (modelo numbers: unverified). The Impuesto Complementario expense is not deductible in IS (LIS art. 15.b). This is not the same as art. 30 bis.
